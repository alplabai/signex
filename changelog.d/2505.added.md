- **Place a component directly on the board (#2505)** — Place ▸ Component... on a board, the Component... row of the board's right-click Place menu and the PCB Active Bar's Components button now open the part picker instead of being greyed "not available on boards yet". The picked part's footprint follows the cursor; Space turns it, L (Flip Selection) moves it to the other side, and each click places one copy as one undo step with the next designator. A footprint placed this way is not linked to a schematic part, so Update PCB lists it as kept unlinked and never removes it.
