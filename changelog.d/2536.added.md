- **Editable built-in profiles (#2536)** — hovering a tier card shows a pencil, **Edit profile…**: the values you save are kept as your own override of that built-in (same id, tagged **edited**), and **Reset to default** in its menu brings the shipped values back. Saved profiles get a menu with Edit profile…, **Rename**, **Duplicate** and Delete. Projects keep the values they copied.
