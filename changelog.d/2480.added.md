- **The board view is kept between launches (#2480)** — what the Layers panel sets for a board (hidden layers, the active layer, Single Layer, View From Bottom Side, Used On, the layer set, the Connection Lines, Multi-Layer and Origin Marker eyes, hidden kinds of object and their transparencies) is saved in your preferences, per board, and comes back when the board opens again. Every greyed control in the panel and its more menu now says in its tooltip why it cannot be used; Delete current set... asks you to show a saved set first, and Layer Stack... gives the command's own reason.
