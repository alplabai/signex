- **Block diagram from the sheet hierarchy (#2968)** — `generate_block_diagram` writes a `.snxblk` with one block per sheet, linked to the sheet by its document id, its sheet entries as ports, and one connection per signal group that crosses sheets (a bus, a harness, a power net, or nets grouped by their prefix, labelled with the protocol). `regenerate_block_diagram` lists what the hierarchy changed (a new sheet adds one block, a renamed sheet moves its link, a removed sheet leaves its block unlinked) and applies it with `apply=true`, keeping the layout and every hand-drawn block and connection. Both run from the palette and headless from `signex-cli` with `--save`.
