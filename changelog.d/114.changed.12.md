- **Graphite Active Bar and zoom pill (#114)** — on Graphite themes the Active Bar and its dropdowns use the raised fill, a 10 px radius, 7 px buttons, token hover and selected washes and the themed shadow, and the schematic bar floats 10 px below the tab strip. A zoom pill (`-`, the zoom percentage, `+`, Fit) sits in the canvas's bottom-right corner and sends the View menu's Zoom In, Zoom Out and Fit All. Every other theme looks exactly as before.
