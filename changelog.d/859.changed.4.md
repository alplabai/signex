- **Annotate and Reset Duplicate Designators no longer freeze the window (#859)** — the project's sheets are read on a background job, the open sheets are numbered in one step (still one undo step per sheet, or one for the project), and the sheets that are not open are written by a second job, with the same designators as before. While a run reads, its declared pages and root are held; while it writes, the unopened sheets are. Rename, delete, open and save of a held sheet are refused, and a second run asked for meanwhile is refused with a notice. If anything was opened, saved, closed, renamed or deleted, or a sheet symbol placed or removed, while the sheets were being read, the run changes nothing and says so. A sheet that cannot be written is named in the Messages panel and in a notice; the others still land.
