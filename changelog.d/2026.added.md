- **Project schematic defaults (#2026)** — Project ▸ Schematic defaults sets the wire, bus, symbol line and pin, shape and junction widths, the wire line style, and the text size, font and shape fill new items are placed with; unset items follow them on the canvas, in exports, in hit testing and in selection boxes. They are saved in the `.snxprj`, undoable, and an existing file looks the same until you change them.
