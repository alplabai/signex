- **Project schematic defaults (#2026)** — Project ▸ Schematic defaults sets the wire, bus, symbol line and pin, shape and junction widths, the wire line style, the shape fill new shapes are placed with, and the text size and font of labels and notes placed by click; unset items follow them on the canvas and in exports, and wires and buses are also hit and boxed at those widths. They are saved in the `.snxprj`, undoable, and an existing file looks the same until you change them.
