- **Board graphics tools (#2424)** — Place ▸ Graphics on a board and the PCB Active Bar's Graphics group draw lines, arcs (centre, start, end), full circles, rectangles and text frames (text wrapped in a box) on the active non-copper layer, one undo step each. Properties shows what the next click places (Tab pauses to edit it) and edits a placed drawing's layer, width, points, text and text height; drawings are picked on their stroke and deleted like other board items. They are saved with the board, printed, and written to the fabrication outputs on fabrication layers. Agents add and edit drawings with `add_board_graphic` and `set_board_graphic`.
