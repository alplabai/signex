- **Layout plan stored with the board (#2942)** — a board keeps the settings of its layout stages (placement weights, rooms and parts to keep; nets and net classes to route in order, nets to skip; pours to fill in order; DRC rules and requirements that must pass). `layout_plan_get`, `layout_plan_set` (one undo step per call) and `layout_plan_status` (each stage not started, done or failing, with the reasons) run from `signex-cli` and through a running Signex, and answer with JSON. A board with a plan writes `snxpcb/1.12`, which earlier builds open read-only; a board without one keeps its token.
