- **Part Properties follow the new layout (#2086)** — a selected part opens with a line-art drawing of the part on the sheet (mirror, pin numbers, net stubs, designator, value; DNP and Locked marks), and a library change shows as a banner at the top with Update from library and Only this one. General lists Designator, Value, Description, Footprint with its Override, Datasheet as a link, Library with its id on one row, Symbol and Body, then the part actions; the ⋯ button holds the same actions plus Copy Library ID. Mirror X, Mirror Y, Locked and DNP sit in a 2×2 grid, the Pins table shows its count in the header and keeps a hidden pin's state in the row tooltip, and the footprint thumbnail is drawn as line art.
- **A clicked pin opens with its facts first (#2086)** — clicking a pin of a placed part keeps the part selected and shows the pin's drawing with its read-only Designator, Name, Type, Net, Length, Orientation and Hidden, plus Edit in Symbol Editor and Show net (the Net is a link to the Net Inspector). The other part sections fold while the pin is focused and come back as you left them afterwards.
