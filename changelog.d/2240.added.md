- **Place footprints on a board (#2240)** — on a PCB tab you can select a footprint by its body (its courtyard, or its pads when it has none) with a click, Shift/Ctrl-click or a box, drag it with a preview that snaps its origin to the board grid, and rotate, flip or delete it. Each edit is one undo step. The rotate and mirror keys, the new Edit ▸ Move submenu, the Active Bar rotate and flip actions, the palette and a new board right-click menu (Move Selection, Rotate, Rotate Clockwise, Flip Selection, Delete) all act on the board; Edit ▸ Move carries footprints from one click to the next. A locked footprint refuses every edit with a notice in the Messages panel, and an undocked PCB window selects and edits its own board.
