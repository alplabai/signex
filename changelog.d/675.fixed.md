- **ERC and the netlist now agree on power ports and pin contact (#675)** — same-value power ports on separate wires join into one net (no more `GND` plus `GND_2`) and name it for ERC rules, and pins of two different parts touching tip to tip count as connected, so a power port dropped on a pin no longer raises a false unconnected-pin warning or drops out of the exported netlist.
