- **Board selection follows one set of rules (#2396)** — a plain click on empty board space now clears the selection, whether one item or many is selected; the ratsnest lines are empty space. A plain click on an item selects only it, a Shift or Ctrl click toggles it and keeps the rest, a box from empty space replaces the selection (Shift adds, Ctrl toggles) and a box that catches nothing clears it unless Shift or Ctrl is held. A Shift or Ctrl released while another window had the focus no longer sticks and turns later clicks into toggles.
- **Board tools get every click (#2396)** — while Track, Via, Fill, Region or Keepout is armed, or a Move, paste or part waits on the cursor, every press on the board is a click for it: a press on a footprint no longer drags it and a slightly moved click no longer draws a selection box. Arming a tool also drops a guide waiting to be placed.
