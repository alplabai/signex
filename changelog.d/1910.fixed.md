- **In an undocked schematic window, the Active Bar, its dropdowns and the right-click menu act on that window's sheet (#1910)** — they used to act on the sheet open in the main window, and a dropdown or menu opened in one window could also paint in another. Each now acts on, and paints in, the window it was opened in. Move Selection by X, Y and Find Similar opened from such a window also finish on that window's sheet. Known gap: the menu entries Properties and Find still use the main window's sheet.
