- **Components panel data model (#2785)** — data rate (`bps` with k, M and G) and °C are units; a min-to-max value such as supply voltage or temperature is two measurements that a class template pairs into one value, which sorts by its chosen end and filters on Covers, Min or Max; a range filter on Any prefix compares the displayed number. A class template can set the columns, nested sort, grouping and pinned parameters its category opens with, and the bundled capacitor and resistor templates do; the panel that shows them follows.
