- **Paste through the system clipboard and re-designate pasted parts (#1263)** — Copy and Cut now also put the selection on the OS clipboard as text (a `signex-clipboard-fragment/1` line followed by the fragment in the `.snxsch` format, library symbol bodies included), so another Signex window or process can paste it; clipboard text that is not a fragment is ignored and one that cannot be read is reported in the Messages panel. The new Designators on paste preference in Preferences > Editing keeps the copied designators (default), resets them to `R?`, `U?`, ..., or gives each part the next unused number of its prefix in the project, for Paste, Smart Paste, Duplicate, rubber-stamp copy and Paste Array; the units of one part always stay one part.
