- **Compare the board with the schematic from the command line (#2462)** — `check_board_against_schematic` answers every difference between a project's board and its schematic as JSON, each with an id, its two sides and the fixes it offers, and says when the board matches. `apply_board_schematic_differences` takes differences by id to the board or to the schematic through the Compare Board with Schematic dialog's Apply: one board undo step and one schematic undo group.
