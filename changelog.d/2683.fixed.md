- **Footprint pattern pitches, counts, pad rotation and polar sweep no longer read SI multipliers (#2683)** — a pitch of `2.54m` is 2.54 metres, as in every other length field, instead of 0.00254 mm that stacked every pad on one spot; a count, a pad rotation or a polar sweep written as `4k7`, `1k` or `10m` is refused as an error instead of baking 4700 pads or a 4700° turn.
