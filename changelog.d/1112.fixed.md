- **A save under one spelling can no longer land over a newer save under another (#1112)** — the write-ordering ledger, the in-flight set and the Save As refusal were keyed by the exact path, so on a case-insensitive volume `Lib.snxsch` and `lib.snxsch` were two files to the ledger: an older snapshot could overwrite a newer one after the dirty marker was cleared, and the older write's completion could clear the newer one's marker. They are now keyed by a case-folded path that does not depend on the project or its volume probe, so a file keeps one ordering slot however it is spelled. Two case-twin files on a case-sensitive volume share a slot too; the older write is then skipped, that document stays modified, and the Messages panel says so.
