- **A save under one spelling can no longer land over a newer save under another (#1112)** — the write-ordering ledger, the in-flight set and Save As refusal were keyed by the exact path, so on a case-insensitive volume `Lib.snxsch` and `lib.snxsch` were two files to the ledger: an older snapshot could overwrite a newer one after the dirty marker was cleared, and the older write's completion could clear the newer one's marker. They are now keyed by the owning project's probed volume fold key, the same one the sheet and library lookups use.
