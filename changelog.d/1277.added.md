- **Net ties (#1277)** — a part can be a net tie: it joins two named nets on purpose, which stay two nets, so ERC reports no short, and Update PCB makes its footprint a net-tie copper bridge that DRC allows. A part placed from a Net Tie library symbol is one; `set_net_tie` and `query_net_ties` set and list them from the CLI, and the netlist listing carries each tie.
