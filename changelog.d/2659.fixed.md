- **Sheet entry sync knows `Repeat(<net>)` entries (#2659)** — Synchronize Sheet Entries and Ports and Sync Sheet Pins match a `Repeat(VOUT)` entry with the child sheet's port `VOUT`, so they no longer offer a second, plain `VOUT` entry or drop the repeat entry. A renamed port keeps the entry a repeat (`Repeat(VOUT_5V)`), and Symbol to Sheet adds the plain port for a new repeat entry.
