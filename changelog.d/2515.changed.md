- **Copper of no net in the design rule check and the router (#2515)** — Tracks, pads, copper arcs and vias with no net, and copper text and drawings, are now checked against every net's copper (a short or clearance finding) and against the board edge clearance rule, where the check used to skip them. The interactive router routes around them and a push never moves a track of no net. They still connect nothing: a track of no net across two pads of one net is a short to each and the pads stay unrouted. Boards with copper of no net near other copper or the board edge can show new findings.
