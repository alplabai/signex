- **Find and Replace: match options, include boxes and a new layout (#2525)** — Match case, Whole word and Regular expression toggles sit beside the Find field (an invalid expression marks the field and finds nothing). In Replace mode every result has an include box, on by default, and shows its result inline (`VOUT → VOUT_3V3`); the primary button reads "Replace 1 of 5" and replaces only the included rows, as one undo step. The dialog is wider, switches between Find and Replace in its header, shows the match count inside the Find field, groups the results by kind with the sheet beside each, and says "Match 2 of 5" in the footer.
