- **A new board counts from the bottom-left corner of its board area, Y up (#2384)** — the origin marker, the status bar, Properties X/Y, guides, grids and the sheet all read from the board's origin with Y increasing upward. New boards (and the New PCB setup) start with the origin at the bottom-left of the board area, and Update PCB stages parts inside an empty board from that corner. Boards saved before keep their origin.
