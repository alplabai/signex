- **Board options in the board Properties panel (#2374)** — The sections #2353 showed greyed out now work. The Guide Manager adds vertical, horizontal, +45° and -45° guides (Add puts one through the origin, Place sets one where you click on the board), edits their X / Y, enabled flag and colour, and deletes them; enabled guides are drawn across the board view and pull a moved footprint while Snap Options ▸ Guides is on. Snap Options ▸ Axes lines a move up with object points within the Axis Snap Range. The Objects for snapping list adds Track/Arc Lines, Arc Centers, Intersections, Pad Vertices and Pad Edges (3D Body Snap Points stays off: boards hold no 3D bodies). The Grid Manager adds, edits (Properties: name, step, origin), recolours and deletes extra snap grids that apply to components, non-components or both, drawn over the board in their colour; its Fast Grids buttons set the board's step to a fast grid. Other edits the Polygon Naming Scheme, Designator Display and Sheet Settings (X, Y, Width, Height, Get Size From Sheet Layer); the sheet is drawn behind the board. Guides, grids, the sheet and the naming options are saved in the board file and every edit is one undo step. Still greyed: the Selection Filter kinds the board cannot pick yet (#2373), the Parameters tab, Board Information arcs and fills, and Reports.
