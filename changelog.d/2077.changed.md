- **A drag step derives only the nets the move changed (#2077)** — the drag ghost keeps the connectivity of the sheet as it was before the move and, for each cursor step, rebuilds only the wires, dots, labels, pins and ports the move can reach, instead of deriving every net of the sheet several times; the nets, and so the ghost and the committed move, are unchanged. A sheet the shortcut cannot judge (its library symbols changed, or two elements share an id) is derived in full. Each step also checks again only the junction dots near the wires that moved, and with few checks to make it no longer builds a spatial index of every wire (also faster when the move is committed). On a 480-symbol sheet a drag step takes about 4.8 ms, down from 13 ms.
