- **Chassis-ground power port (#1284)** — a new Chassis power-port style, drawn as the frame/chassis symbol (IEC 60417-5020): a bar with three slanted tines. Place it from the Active Bar's power list ("Place Chassis power port") or pick Chassis in the Style row of a power port's Properties; it draws the same on the canvas, in SVG and in PDF, and saves as `power:Chassis`.
