- **Copper text and copper shapes on a board (#2883)** — String, Full Circle, Rectangle and Text Frame now place on a copper layer, where they used to place nothing and say why in the status bar. A string on copper is real copper text, and a circle, rectangle or text frame on copper is a copper outline, all with no net. The design rule check reports a short or clearance finding between them and other nets' copper, polygon pours keep their clearance from them, the interactive router and via stitching, teardrops and gloss keep clear of them, and the Gerber writes them as conductor copper. Keep-out layers still take no drawing.
