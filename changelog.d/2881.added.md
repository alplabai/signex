- **Drawing order and Delete run by uuid from the CLI and agents (#2326)** — `bring_drawings_forward`, `bring_drawings_to_front`, `send_drawings_backward`, `send_drawings_to_back` and `delete_selection` take `document` and `ids`, so a script or an agent can reorder or delete the drawings and items it names on an open sheet or board. A uuid that names nothing, or a document that is not open, is refused with a reason. The key, menu and palette act on the selection exactly as before. Delete on a footprint editor tab is unchanged.
