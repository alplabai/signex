- **Pour, string and dimension properties (#2523)** — a polygon pour can be shelved: it keeps its outline and settings, holds no copper, draws as a dashed outline and is saved with the board. Shelve, Unshelve and Repour sit on the pour's Copper card, with Repour all, Shelve all and Unshelve all in its menu; each shelve is one undo step. The pour panel gains a line drawing, Fill Mode, and the thermal relief and corner settings shown greyed until the fill uses them. A string's text comes first across the panel, with quick 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° rotation; a dimension shows its value on a card and picks its precision from 0 to 6.
