- **Breaking: `.snxpcb` boards saved by this version are format `snxpcb/1.3` (#680)** — pads may carry a `rotation` column, the `trapezoid` and `custom` pad shapes (which had no parameters and drew as rectangles) are gone, and a pad with an unknown shape or a round-rect corner ratio outside 0 to 0.5 now fails to load, naming the field and value. A build that predates 1.3 opens the file read-only. Re-draw any trapezoid or custom pad as a rect or round-rect before opening the board.
