- **Board keys in both built-in profiles (#2406)** — on a board `1` toggles Board Sketch, `L` flips the selection or the part on the cursor, `F` changes the footprint and `Q` toggles the unit. The chord profile adds `N` (ratsnest), `P K` (keepout) and `T D` (design rule check); Classic adds `C`, `K`, `F7` and `Ctrl+M` (measure). A key bound on a board keeps its sheet meaning on a sheet.
