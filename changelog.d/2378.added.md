- **Track and via lock and mask settings (#2378)** — the Track and Via panels now store and edit Lock, solder and paste mask expansion (Rule or a manual value), via tenting per side and From Hole Edge. A locked track or via takes no edit in its panel until it is unlocked; a drag, delete or align on the board does not check the lock yet. Each change is one undo step and is saved in the board file; a board that sets none saves exactly as before.
