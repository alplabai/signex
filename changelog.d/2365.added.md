- **Board component settings (#2365)** — a placed component stores its Type (Standard, Standard (No BOM), Mechanical, Graphical, Net Tie (No BOM), Net Tie (In BOM), Jumper), Height, 3D Body Opacity, Lock Primitives, Lock Strings, Enable Pin Swapping, Enable Part Swapping, Channel Offset and its library footprint's Description, saved in the `.snxpcb` only when set, so older boards re-save unchanged. The Component view edits each one as one undo step. A component placed from a library footprint takes its type, height and description; Update PCB brings new ones and keeps the board's opacity, locks, swapping and channel offset. Lock Primitives refuses edits of the component's pads, Lock Strings refuses moving its texts, and a Standard (No BOM), Mechanical, Graphical or Net Tie (No BOM) component leaves its part out of the BOM while the board is open, listed only with the not-fitted parts.
