- **Hovering a symbol's body shows the part, not a net (#1958)** — the inside of an unfilled body (the STOCK-R rectangle) belongs to the symbol, so its hover card is the part's. A net card shows only where a click would pick a pin: within the pin pick tolerance of a pin line, never inside the body. Hover, click and right-click share that one rule, with a 4 px radius that keeps pins pickable when the sheet is zoomed far out.
