- **IPC-2581 export (#2484)** — Fabrication Outputs now also writes `<name>-ipc2581.xml`, an IPC-2581 revision C file with the whole job: the stackup, copper, solder mask, paste, legend and document layers, the board outline, drills and slots, packages, placed parts, the BOM and the nets. Each layer's function comes from its layer kind, never its display name; numbered Mechanical layers are document layers that keep their names. Nets whose names differ only by a `:` stay apart, and every part of a panel is a component and a BOM row under its own designator.
