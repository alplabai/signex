- **Power tree from the schematic (#1313)** — `query_power_tree` answers a project's power tree, generated from the schematic: every rail with its voltage (from the project, else the net name), its sources and loads, the current each part draws (`Rail Load <net>`), the source's limit (`Rail Supply <net>`), the total, the headroom and a budget level; each converter with what it delivers and draws, worked out from its outputs and its `Efficiency`; and the power budget per sheet, which is the budget of the block that stands for the sheet. Missing data is named, never guessed. The block diagram tab's Power tree view comes in a later change.
