- **Rules tab: clearance matrix, presets and text view (#2530)** — the Clearance matrix keeps only its lower triangle live, rings the clicked cell and opens its object pairs in a card beside it (an empty pair shows the All objects value, ↺ clears a written one), with a legend and a preview row that follows the hovered or pinned cell. Presets are tier cards with their clearance, width, drill and annular minimums and a preview of each change old → new, with Cancel and Apply in the same card. The text view has line numbers, keyword colours, the rejected line marked, one banner per problem or a green "No problems in the text.", and Apply is off while the text has an error.
