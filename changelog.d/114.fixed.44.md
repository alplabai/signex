- **Graphite dialogs share one shell (#114)** — a dialog in its own window now draws the Graphite title bar (the Signex mark, the title, an optional subtitle and the Graphite close) and shows the Signex mark as its window icon; dragging the bar moves the window, and its close runs the same close as the system one, so unsaved-changes guards still apply. Confirm and notice dialogs open with an icon tile for their kind (warning, error, file, library, settings) above a title line and the explanation; footers end with a quiet Cancel and one primary action at the dense button height, with destructive actions still in the danger style; dialog fields, dropdowns and checkboxes use the Graphite form look. Copy to Library, Close Library and Delete Component no longer leave an empty band above their footer, and the library dialogs sit on the themed dialog scrim.
