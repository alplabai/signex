- **Wire, label, power port and text note Properties (#2523)** — each panel opens with a line-art drawing of the object, named after its fields, and keeps one section order: Location, Properties, Net, Net Parameters, Style, then State. A wire's or bus's Location holds its whole segment: Start and End are both editable (one undo step each) and Length follows them. A wire's Width is a 2 x 2 switch. A net label names its justification anchor beside the grid; a global port asks for its Port Name. A power port picks its style from eight glyph tiles, shows Power Net and High Speed side by side with High Speed and Differential Pair greyed while Power Net is on, and can now be locked. A text note's Text is a multi-line box that grows to six lines and applies as one edit (Ctrl+Enter, leaving the box, or selecting something else); its Rotation and Justify H / V are switches you can change, and its Text Size is editable.
