- **Wire, label, power port and text note Properties (#2523)** — each panel opens with a line-art drawing of the object, named after its fields, and keeps one section order: Location, Properties, Net, Net Parameters, Style, then State. A wire's or bus's Location holds its whole segment: Start and End are one X / Y row each. Typing Start moves only the start point and typing End moves only the end point; the other end stays where it is, nothing attached follows, and a straight wire may turn. Each typed value is one undo step, and Length follows both ends. A wire's Width is a 2 x 2 switch. A net label names its justification anchor beside the grid; a global port asks for its Port Name. A power port picks its style from eight glyph tiles, shows Power Net and High Speed side by side with High Speed and Differential Pair greyed while Power Net is on, and can now be locked. A text note's Text is a multi-line box that grows to six lines and applies as one edit when the box loses focus (Escape or a click outside it), on Ctrl+Enter, or when something else is selected; moving the pointer out of the box applies nothing. Text typed into a note is applied to its own sheet before a save, a tab switch or a tab close, so it is never lost. Its Rotation and Justify H / V are switches you can change, and its Text Size is editable.
