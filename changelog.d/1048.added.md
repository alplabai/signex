- Buses now carry the signals their label names. One shared parser reads `D[0..7]`, `D[7..0]`, `D[7:0]` and `{SDA SCL}` groups and refuses malformed text. A bus run takes its members from its labels without ever merging them, and ERC gains five warnings: a bus-entry wire with no label, a label that is not a member of its bus (such as `D33`), a bus with no valid name, two names on one bus that carry different signals, and a bus-name label that sits on a wire instead of a bus. A bus-name label now counts as on its bus anywhere along it, for the orphan-label and hierarchical-port checks as well as the width check (#1048).
