- Buses now carry the signals their label names. One shared parser reads `D[0..7]`, `D[7..0]`, `D[7:0]`, `D[0..3]A` and `{SDA SCL}` groups and refuses malformed text. The netlist can list the member nets of a bus without ever merging them, and ERC gains three warnings: a bus-entry wire with no label, a label that is not a member of its bus (such as `D33`), and a bus with no name. A bus-name label now counts as on its bus anywhere along it, for the orphan-label and hierarchical-port checks as well as the width check (#1048).
