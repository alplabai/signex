- **Block diagram file format (#2796)** — a new `.snxblk` document (`snxblk/1`) holds a block diagram: blocks with their function, rails, key specs, shape and colour, ports, signal-flow connections labelled with their interface (SPI, I2C, UART, USB, LVDS, power, analog or any other name), groups, and the link from a block to its schematic sheet. The writer is deterministic and the format round-trips in the fixture corpus. The document tab comes in a later change.
