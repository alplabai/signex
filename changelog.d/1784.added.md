- **Parameter Manager across sheets (#1784)** — an Active sheet / All sheets switch at the top of the dialog lists the parts of every open sheet of the project, with a Sheet column, and edits them in place; a note beside the switch counts the project's sheets that are not open and so not listed. Adding or removing a column, or Revert changes, across several sheets is one undo step: one Ctrl+Z takes it back on every sheet. The dialog opens on Active sheet. A part on a child sheet placed more than once is named by the designator of the placement its tab shows, as on the canvas and in the Object List.
