- **Parameter Manager across sheets (#1784)** — an Active sheet / All sheets switch at the top of the dialog lists the parts of every sheet of the project, with a Sheet column, and edits them in place. Closed sheets are read from disk and listed read-only after the open ones, and exported to and planned against by the CSV import too; a press on one of their cells opens the sheet, and an edit across sheets that would change a closed sheet changes nothing and says which sheets to open. Adding or removing a column, or Revert changes, across several sheets is one undo step: one Ctrl+Z takes it back on every sheet. The dialog opens on Active sheet. A part on a child sheet placed more than once is named by the designator of the placement its tab shows, as on the canvas and in the Object List.
