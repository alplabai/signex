- **MCAD kernel bring-up (#2487)** — a new `signex-mcad` crate builds solid bodies on an exact B-rep kernel: the board body is the outline extruded to the board thickness with its holes and cut-outs cut through, its faces keep persistent names through the cuts, a failed kernel operation comes back as an error instead of a crash, and solids tessellate into triangle meshes for the 3D view. It does no IO and also builds for WebAssembly. The board STEP export (#2488) is its first user. Every release package (.deb, AppImage, tarball, .dmg, Windows installer and ZIP) now carries LICENSE, LICENSE-APACHE, NOTICE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, which hold the kernel's NOTICE.
