- **Breaking: headless `undo`, `redo`, `save_document`, `save_document_as`, `close_active_document`, `color_net` and `clear_all_net_colors` need `document=<id or path>` (#2172)** — a headless run names the document it acts on and no longer falls back to the active one; `color_net` and `clear_all_net_colors` take `document` in place of the optional `path`. The palette and keybindings keep the active document.
