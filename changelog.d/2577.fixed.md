- **Half-typed pad values no longer clear the pad (#2577)** — in the footprint pad form and the next-pad defaults, text that is not a number in a drill, slot length, paste or mask expansion, copper offset or hole tolerance field used to clear the stored value or reset it to the rule. It is now refused: the pad keeps its value, the text stays in the field with a red outline and the reason, and an empty field still clears an optional value. The sketch pad form refuses a copper offset, hole tolerance or expansion the same way. Undo and redo drop refused text, and a copper offset or tolerance edit is one undo step per field.
