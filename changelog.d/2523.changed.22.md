- **Via and pad properties (#2523)** — a via now picks one of the board's via types, which sets its span, sizes and tenting in one step; Override lets one via keep its own diameter and drill. A via that already differs from its type shows Override on, with its own values in bold and a reset arrow each; the stack tiles, Diameter and Drill stay greyed until Override is on. A Protection section names the via's IPC-4761 type and keeps Tented Top and Bottom editable per via, and small line drawings show the via over its layers and the pad with its openings. A microvia type lists Stacked / Staggered greyed under Not stored yet. A surface pad shows only its own copper layer, without stack choices or a hole.
