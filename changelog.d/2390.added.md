- **Board 3D view (#2390)** — View ▸ 3D View, `3` on a board, or the command palette shows the board in 3D: the outline extruded to the board's thickness with its drill holes, cutouts and mounting holes through it, the copper on both faces, and each component as its library footprint's attached STEP or VRML model, placed by position, rotation and side, or as a plain body extruded from its 3D Body outline. Drag to turn, right-drag to slide, scroll to zoom; Top, Bottom, Front, Left, Right, Angled and Fit sit on the view's bar, and Fit All frames it too. A click selects the part's footprint, the same selection as the flat view. Drawn on the GPU through the existing renderer; nothing in the board changes, so there is no undo step and no dirty mark.
