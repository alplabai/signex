- **Placed ICs show their pin numbers and names (#1961)** — the sheet drew only bare pin lines for placed symbols; each pin now shows its number beside the line and its name inside the body, following the symbol's show-numbers / show-names settings and each pin's own flags, on the CPU and GPU canvas and in SVG/PDF export. Vertical pins' text is turned 90 degrees. Stock resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, LEDs, crystals, fuses and ferrites now hide their pin numbers, and stock transistors hide their pin names.
