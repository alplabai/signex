- **The layer tabs show every shown layer** — the strip under the board lists a tab for every layer the Layers panel shows (copper, the component layer pairs, Board Outline, Margin, Dimensions, Notes, ECO 1 and 2, Drill Drawing, the board's mechanical layers and Multi-Layer), in the panel's order, and scrolls sideways when they do not fit. Hiding a layer takes its tab away; showing it brings the tab back. Before, the strip listed only the few layers a board file declares.
- **Overlay is called Silkscreen** — Top Overlay and Bottom Overlay are Top Silkscreen and Bottom Silkscreen in the Layers panel, the tabs, the menus and the layer pickers; the short tab names stay TO and BO. Board files are unchanged.
- **The bottom layer of every pair is a darker shade of the top** — silkscreen, solder, paste, keep-out and the component layer pairs draw their bottom layer in the top's hue at 80 % of its lightness, so the two sides are told apart at a glance. Bottom copper keeps its own blue.
- **The board's right-click menu follows what is clicked** — on an empty area with nothing selected it is a short menu: Find, Place, Undo, Redo, Paste, Paste Special, Select All, Board Sketch, the reuse block rows and Toggle Ratsnest. On an object or a selection the editing rows follow, each only for the kinds it acts on: Change Footprint, Swap Pins and Swap Gates for a footprint, Lock, Unlock, Gloss Selected Routes and Add Teardrops for a track or via, Select Net and Highlight Net for an object with a net.
- **A double-click closes a polygon** — Polygon Pour, Pour Cutout, Solid Region and Keepout close the shape on a double-click, as well as on a click on the first corner or a right-click.
- **Properties comes to the front when a board tool is armed** — arming a board placement tool that has a form shows Properties on what the next click places, without taking the keyboard from the canvas.
