- **The Properties panel follows the interface language (#2059)** — section titles, row labels, buttons, tooltips and most pick-list entries of the Properties panel (Document Options, the Tab placement form and a selected part, pin, field, label, text note, power port, drawing or sheet symbol), the colour swatch field, the net class rows and the footprint card now come from the message catalog; the Symbol and Footprint lists of a placed part still show their extra entries in English. The rotation, body, power-port style, wire width, net class line, font, sheet colour, orientation and origin lists pick by value, not by their shown text. The held-board notice and the ERC report refusal now render their nested message in the current language. English text is unchanged.
