- **ODB++ export (#2485)** — Fabrication Outputs now also writes `<name>-odb.tar`, an ODB++ job with the stack order, copper, solder mask, paste, legend and document layers, the board profile, drills and slots with their tools, the placed components and the nets. Each layer's type comes from its layer kind, never its display name; numbered Mechanical layers are document layers that keep their names. The same job can be written as a folder instead of an archive.
