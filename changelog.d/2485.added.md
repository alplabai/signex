- **ODB++ export (#2485)** — Fabrication Outputs now also writes `<name>-odb.tar`, an ODB++ job with the stack order, copper, solder mask, paste, legend and document layers, the board profile, drills and slots with their tools, the placed components and the nets. Each layer's type comes from its layer kind, never its display name; numbered Mechanical layers are document layers that keep their names. A slot or cutout drawn on the board outline layer is written on the job's rout layer. Each part and net keeps a name of its own in the job, so a panel's copies of one designator stay apart. An old IPC-2581 or ODB++ file left by an earlier run is reported as stale.
