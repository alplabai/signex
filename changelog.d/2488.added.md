- **Board STEP export (#2488)** — File ▸ Export ▸ STEP 3D Model... on a board writes the board and its parts as a STEP model for MCAD tools. The board body is an exact solid from the MCAD kernel: the outline extruded to the board thickness with its cut-outs, mounting holes, pad drills and via drills cut through, hole walls as true cylinders and each face named (Top, Bottom, Hole 3 wall). Each part is the body the 3D view shows, placed at its footprint and named by its designator. AP214 by default, AP242 on request; the command-line tool writes one with `export_board_step`. Signex's STEP importer now also follows edges a face loop runs backwards, so such faces read back with their real outline.
