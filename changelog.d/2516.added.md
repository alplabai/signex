- **45° drag wiring (#2516)** — `cycle_wiring_mode` now also steps to 45° first and 45° last, after Straight: the wires that follow a dragged part leave their fixed end on a 45° leg and then turn onto the pin, or the other way round, with the same corners as the wire tool's 45° modes. A blocked route takes the 45° leg at the other end. The status bar names the mode and the ghost shows what the drop commits.
