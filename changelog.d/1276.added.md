- **Bottom-up hierarchy (#1276)** — Design ▸ Create Sheet Symbol From Sheet... places a sheet symbol for a chosen `.snxsch` with an entry for every port, as one undo step. Design ▸ Synchronize Sheet Entries and Ports... syncs a sheet symbol and its sheet in either direction: the dialog lists the entries or ports that would be added, removed and renamed, and Apply makes exactly those changes as one undo step. A renamed entry keeps its place and its wires, and only the chosen sheet symbol's generated entries are renamed, never a hand-placed one; a sheet that is not open is changed in memory and left unsaved. Both rows are also in the sheet menu's Sheet Actions.
