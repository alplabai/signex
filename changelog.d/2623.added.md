- **3D view keys (#2623)** — on a board in the 3D view, `H` steps to the next lighting preset, `Ctrl+Shift+C` (`Cmd+Shift+C` on macOS) copies a screenshot and `F` turns the board over, in both built-in keyboard profiles; the view's tooltips, screenshot menu and lighting toast name these keys. `F` still changes the footprint on a flat board, where the lighting and copy commands are greyed because they need the 3D view.
