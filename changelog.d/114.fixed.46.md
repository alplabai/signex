- **Graphite Active Bar icons and filter chips (#114)** — on the Graphite themes the power-port button draws the schematic GND glyph (stem on top, three shrinking bars) instead of a standby symbol, and switches to a rail glyph after a VCC or rail port is placed; the wire button draws a routed wire instead of a pulse, Directives draws the No ERC cross on a wire end, Sheet Symbol draws a box with sheet entries, and Net Color draws a swatch instead of a plus. The Selection Filter's categories are toggle chips (on: soft accent fill, accent ink and hairline; off: transparent with a line hairline), "All - On" is an All / None segmented control, and the net-colour swatches and rows use the Graphite swatch and hover.
