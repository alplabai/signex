- **ERC markers follow the sheet, and Paste no longer duplicates designators (#1768)** — the ERC panel, the canvas markers, F8 and the exported report leave out a finding whose part, wire or label is no longer on the sheet, and Undo brings it back; the last run's results are kept as they were and the panel says "Results may be out of date" after any edit until ERC runs again (a moved or re-split wire can still show or hide a finding until then). Marker rings and crosses keep contrast on light and dark sheets with faint fills, so the symbol stays visible. The ERC panel opens tall enough for several rows, with the location on one line and the full path in a tooltip. A pasted, duplicated or stamped part whose designator is already used takes the next free number (R2 pasted becomes R3), a free designator is kept, `R?` stays `R?`, and the units of a multi-unit part stay together. "Designators on paste" gains "Keep unless taken" as its default; a saved "Keep" from an earlier build is moved to it once (noted in the Messages panel), and a Keep chosen afterwards stays.
