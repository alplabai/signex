- **Board String, Arc, Pad and Dimension tools (#2373)** — on a board, Place and the PCB Active Bar now run String, Arc, Pad and Dimension. A string goes on an overlay, mechanical or documentation layer. An arc is drawn on the active layer by centre, by edge or at any angle (Cycle Arc Mode in the palette); on copper it carries the net it starts on and counts as copper for the ratsnest, the design rule check and fabrication outputs. A pad placed on its own is a through-hole pad on the net under it. A linear dimension measures two points and stands off at a third. Each placement is one undo step, each has its own Properties panel, and free arcs, pads and dimensions are saved with the board.
