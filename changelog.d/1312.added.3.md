- **Cross probe between the schematic and the board (#1312)** — Tools ▸ Cross Probe, the Cross Probe row of the sheet and board canvas menus, and the command palette (`cross_probe`) select the part or net you picked on the other side, bringing that tab forward or opening the project's board or the part's sheet, and zoom to it. Tools ▸ Cross Select Mode (on by default; Ctrl+Shift+X in the Altium preset) selects every pick on the other side as you make it, in the main window and undocked windows, without opening anything or taking focus. Parts are matched by the footprint's schematic link (sheet and symbol), not by designator; nets by name.
