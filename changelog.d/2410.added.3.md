- **Pin and gate swap, and design reuse blocks, on a board (#2410)** — Swap Pins and Swap Gates on the board right-click menu trade the nets of two equivalent pins, or of two equivalent gates, of a component, as the part's Pin Swap Groups / Gate Swap Groups parameters allow; the linked schematic is rewired in the same undo step. Save as Reuse Block saves the selected placed and routed parts as a block of the project (`reuse-blocks/<name>.snxpcb`), and the board menu's Place Reuse Block rows lay a block onto another channel of the same circuit around the selected component, tracks and vias included, as one undo step. All four are commands with operands for the CLI and agents (`swap_pins`, `swap_gates`, `save_board_reuse_block`, `place_board_reuse_block`) that answer with what changed.
