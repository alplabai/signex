- **Pinch to zoom (#2017)** — Pinching on a trackpad zooms the schematic, PCB, symbol and footprint canvases around the cursor.
- **Two-finger scroll pans (#2017)** — A new opt-in "Two-finger scroll pans" preference makes a trackpad scroll pan the canvas instead of zooming it; the mouse wheel and Ctrl/Cmd+scroll still zoom.
- **Remember window size and position (#2017)** — A new opt-in preference restores the main window to its last size and position, and falls back to the primary monitor if that screen is gone.
