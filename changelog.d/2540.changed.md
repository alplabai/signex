- **Dragging a part off a junction stretches a stub instead of pulling the net's wires (#2540)** — when a moved pin sits on a junction of two or more wires, those wires and their dot stay where they are and a stub grows or shrinks from the junction to the pin, in every drag wiring mode, Auto included. A part's own moving wires no longer block its other pin's path, so such a drag no longer falls back to a plain move. Only when no stub fits do the wires follow the pin, as before.
