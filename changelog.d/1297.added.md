- **Parametric part search and part swap from the command line (#1297)** — `search_parts` finds library parts by the Components panel's search words and by value, package, tolerance, rating and stock, with each part's lifecycle state and cached offers, and never fetches a price. `change_component` with `document`, `id`, `library` and `part` swaps a placed part for the found one as the Change Component dialog does, in one undo step.
