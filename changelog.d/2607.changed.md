- **The Bill of Materials dialog is laid out for the job (#2607)** — the variant is picked once, in the toolbar, beside a Grouped / Ungrouped / Flat switch, a **Filter lines** field (it only hides lines on screen, never from the file) and a chip such as "2 warnings" that opens the Rules tab. Lines a validation rule flags are tinted with a mark in the line-number column, and clicking an issue in the Rules tab selects its line. Numbers are right-aligned in the mono face, the sorted column shows an arrow, and the header stays in view while the lines scroll. **Include DNP** and **Include Not Fitted** are now one option, **Include not-fitted (DNP), excluded-from-BOM and off-board parts**, which lists every part a BOM leaves out. The footer reads "12 of 12 lines · 31 parts" plus the price note, and its buttons are **Cancel** and **Export CSV…** (or XLSX / HTML, after the chosen format). **File ▸ Export ▸ Export BOM…** opens the same dialog.
