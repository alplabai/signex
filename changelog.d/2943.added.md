- **Rail budgets and the board's layer count as project requirements (#2943)** — a project can set each power rail's voltage and tolerance (`project_set_rail`), and a part names the current it draws from a rail or can supply to it with its `Rail Load <net>` and `Rail Supply <net>` parameters. The new requirement kinds `rail_budget` (the rail's voltage matches and its summed load is within the source's supply less a margin) and `layer_count` (the board's copper layers within a minimum and a maximum) pass or fail with their evidence in `check_requirements` and the Project tab, and `query_rail_budget` answers every rail with its voltage, source, loads, total and headroom. A rail or a part with missing data is a finding, never a pass. Projects that set no rail are written as before.
