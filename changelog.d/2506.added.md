- **Unfill polygon pours (#2506)** — Tools ▸ Unfill Selected, a pour's right-click, the Active Bar's Copper Areas group and the Unfill button on a pour's Copper card clear the copper of the selected pours, keeping their outline, as one undo step. An unfilled pour holds no copper and clears nothing on the canvas, in Gerber and the other fabrication outputs, the ratsnest and stitching, keeps a solid outline, and counts as not filled in Fabrication Outputs and the Polygon Manager. Repour fills it again, as one undo step; a Repour of filled pours stays a refresh with no undo step. The state is saved with the board (`unfilled = true`), and a board with an unfilled pour writes `snxpcb/1.11`, so older builds open it read-only. From the command line or an agent, `repour_all` needs the edit right and is recorded in the agent journal, since it can fill unfilled pours.
