- **Fab-profiled DRC (#2311)** — the Standard, Advanced and HDI tiers now carry a full capability set (clearance, track, via, hole size, annular ring, hole spacing, board edge, mask sliver and silkscreen to pad) and say where their numbers come from; a rule profile can name its maker, process, source and date; and a written rule below the `fab_*` floor is applied but reported in Messages and flagged in the Rules tab.
- **A fab tier picked in project setup writes its DRC floor (#2311)** — choosing Standard, Advanced or HDI in New Project, the Project tab's Project setup or New PCB also adds the tier's seven `fab_*` rules to the project's rules, editable like any rule; picking another tier replaces only those rules, in an open project the pick is one undo step and nothing is written until Save. `new_project_with_profile` takes the tier as the `fab` override.
