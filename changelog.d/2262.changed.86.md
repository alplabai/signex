- **Symbol editor Properties, Pins tab (#2262)** — the symbol editor's Pins dock panel is now the **Pins** tab of the Properties panel: a compact list with a filter by number or name, part chips, **+ Pin**, and marks on a duplicate or empty number and an empty name; a row selects its pin, and **‹ Pins** goes back to the list. **Edit pins in table…** switches the list to a table that edits every listed pin's name and Hidden flag in place, as the old panel did. The symbol, pin and graphic forms take the Graphite layout with a line-art illustration whose callouts follow the hovered field, and a rectangle gains a Width / Height pair.
