- **Board Line and Arc draw on copper (#2869)** — the Graphics Line and Arc from the PCB Active Bar now place on a copper layer, where they used to place nothing: Line places straight tracks on the net of the pad, via or track under its first click, and Arc places a track arc on the net under its start. Both count as tracks for the design rule check and Gerber output. Properties shows the track or arc about to be placed. On other layers they still place drawings. The separate Copper Drawing group (Track, Arc) is gone from the bar; Track and the copper Arc tool are still in the Place menu. Full Circle, Rectangle, Text Frame and String still place nothing on copper, and the status bar now says why.
