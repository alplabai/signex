- **Variant Manager: Compare variants and Open in BOM Studio (#2949)** — Compare variants... shows two variants side by side, only the parts they differ on (fit, value, footprint or a field); Open in BOM Studio opens the BOM with the selected variant picked. New commands `compare_variants`, `variant_bom` and `project_set_default_variant` give the agent and the CLI the same comparison, BOM lines and default variant.
