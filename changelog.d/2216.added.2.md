- **Polygon pour follow-ups (#2216)** — a placed pour is picked by a click inside it (the Polygons chip of the Selection Filter is live) and opens the area panel, which adds Priority, Remove Islands Below (mm²), Remove Dead Copper and the copper area and pieces of its fill. New Pour Cutout (cut an area out of the pours on the active copper layer: a hole, a notch or separate pours) and Slice Pour (cut the pours a line crosses in two) tools, each one undo step, on Place and the PCB Active Bar's Copper Areas group. Repour Selected and Repour All (Tools, the Copper Areas group, the palette) fill the board's pours again and say in Messages what they came to; `repour_all document=<board>` answers an agent with each pour's fill as JSON.
