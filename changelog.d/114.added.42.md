- **Mouse wheel: Zoom / Scroll preference (#114)** — Preferences > Appearance gets "Mouse wheel", next to "Two-finger scroll pans". Zoom (default) keeps the wheel zooming. Scroll makes the wheel and a two-finger scroll pan vertically and Shift + wheel pan horizontally; Ctrl/Cmd + wheel and pinch still zoom. It applies to the schematic, PCB, symbol and footprint canvases and is saved with the other preferences.
