- **Interactive routing and length tuning (#2389)** — on a board, Place and the PCB Active Bar now run Interactive Routing. Click a pad or track end, then each point: every leg keeps the clearance the board's design rules ask from other nets, going around their copper, or in push mode (`cycle_route_mode` in the command palette) pushing their tracks aside while keeping them legal. The track width comes from the net class, the corner follows the wire corner modes (Space and Shift+Space), and changing the copper layer adds a via. The whole route is one undo step when it ends (a click on its end, Esc or a right-click). A selected track's Properties panel gets Tune Net To: type a length and the track gains accordion meanders until its net reaches it. If the board changes under a route (an undo mid-route), the route is checked again before it lands and is never dropped without a message. Differential pairs are not routed yet.
