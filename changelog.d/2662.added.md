- **Per-channel variants (#2662)** — in a multi-channel design a variant can fit a part in one channel and leave it out in another. The Variant Manager shows a repeated part as one row ("mixed" where its channels disagree) that opens into one row per channel, and can set every channel at once; the channel cards show each channel's fit in the active variant, grey and cross a channel left out, and leave a whole channel out with one click. The per-variant BOM drops exactly the parts a variant leaves out.
