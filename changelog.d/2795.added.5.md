- **Design Notebook measurements (#2795)** — a `#measurement` records a bench reading against a part, pin or net and prints as a verdict table (Target, Tol., Measured, Error, Result: Pass, Warn or Fail). The readings of one net, part or pin form a timeline that marks failing and stale readings and exports as CSV. `#attachment` figures (scope captures, thermal images, simulation plots) are copied into `notebook/attachments/`, remember the part values they were taken with and are marked stale when one changes; a scope CSV is drawn as a vector plot with its legend, cursors and trigger. New commands: `notebook_measurements`, `notebook_timeline`, `notebook_attachments`, `export_notebook_timeline`, `mark_notebook_attachment_valid` and `attach_notebook_file`.
