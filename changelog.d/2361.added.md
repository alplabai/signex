- **Clipboard on a board (#2361)** — Select All, Cut, Copy, Paste, Paste Special and Duplicate now work on a PCB tab, from the Edit menu, their keys and the board's right-click menu, and grey out with nothing selected or nothing copied. Copy carries footprints with their pads, tracks and vias; Paste follows the pointer on the grid until a click places it; Paste Special and Duplicate place the copy beside the original at once. Each is one undo step. Pasted footprints are renumbered by the paste designator preference when their designator is taken, copper keeps its net only where this board has a net of that name, and a sheet copy cannot be pasted on a board (or the other way round): the Messages panel says why.
