- **Blanket, Compile Mask and Differential Pair directives (#2420)** — the three Directives tools of the Active Bar and the canvas Place flyout now place real objects instead of a "planned" placeholder. A Blanket (two corner clicks) gives every net inside it a net class and stops the ERC rules it names from being reported there; its net class and rules are edited in Properties, each edit one undo step. A Compile Mask takes what it covers out of ERC and the netlist. A Differential Pair directive on a `_P` or `_N` net pairs it with its partner. All three are saved in the `.snxsch` file, can be moved, rotated and deleted like any object, and are also commands with operands (`place_blanket`, `place_compile_mask`, `place_diff_pair`, `edit_blanket`) for scripts and agents. Details: `docs/SCHEMATIC_DIRECTIVES.md`.
