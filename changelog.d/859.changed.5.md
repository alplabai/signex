- **Saving a symbol, footprint or sim no longer stalls the window while the library refreshes, and neither does restoring a library from History (#859)** — after a primitive save the whole library (tables and listings) is re-read on a background job, and the open previews that show the primitive are re-read on another; both are applied when they land. A read that a newer one replaced, or whose library was closed or reopened meanwhile, is dropped, and a read that fails keeps the previous picker entries and says so in the Messages panel.
