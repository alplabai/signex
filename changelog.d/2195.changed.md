- **One PCB layer vocabulary (#2195)** — footprint sketch markers now store their `layer` as the board-layer token (`TopSilkscreen`, `TopCopper`, `Inner2Copper`, `Mechanical13`, …). Per the 2026-11-08 no-legacy-support decision while Signex is in active development: sketch files written before this change no longer load (`top_silk`, `{ inner_copper = 2 }` and every other old spelling fail with an error naming the file, the `layer` attribute and the bad value), and the unsided `KeepOut` and `User(N)` sketch layers are removed. The stock-library footprints are regenerated.
