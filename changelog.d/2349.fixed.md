- **Right-click menus close on a click outside them (#2349)** — a left click outside a context menu closes it and does nothing else; a right click outside closes it and opens the menu of the spot it landed on; Esc closes the canvas, document-tab and Projects-tree menus without resetting the tool. This holds for every right-click menu (canvas, Projects tree, tabs, panels, footprint and symbol editors) and in undocked windows.
