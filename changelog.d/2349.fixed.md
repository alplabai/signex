- **Menus and popups close on a click outside them (#2349)** — a left click outside a context menu closes it and does nothing else; a right click outside closes it and opens the menu of the spot it landed on; Esc closes the canvas, document-tab and Projects-tree menus and the grid picker without resetting the tool. This holds for every right-click menu (canvas, Projects tree, tabs, panels, footprint and symbol editors) and in undocked windows. Active Bar dropdowns, the Panels list, the footprint Move By and Align dialogs and the error and net-colour cards close on an outside click again too.
