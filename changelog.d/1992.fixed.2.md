- **Symbol previews sit on the real sheet (#1992)** — The Components panel and the Component Preview dialog draw a symbol on the schematic paper over the same grid the editor shows (your Visible Grid size and style, 1.27 mm Dots by default), without a sheet border or title block. The preview fits the part to its box when first shown, pans by dragging with the left button, and zooms with Ctrl and the wheel, so a plain wheel still scrolls the panel.
