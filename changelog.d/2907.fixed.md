- **Copper zones cross probe as their net (#2907)** — selecting a polygon pour, fill or solid region on the board now selects its net's wires, labels and sheet entries on the schematic, and selecting a wire, label or sheet entry on the schematic now selects the net's copper zones on the board along with its pads, tracks and vias. Before, a zone was skipped both ways. A keepout or pour cutout still stands for nothing.
