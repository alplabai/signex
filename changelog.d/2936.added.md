- **Board footprints placed by id from the command line (#2936)** — `set_footprint_position`, `set_footprint_rotation` and `set_footprint_side` move, turn and flip a component of a named board, by uuid, designator or a list, in one undo step, and answer with the footprint as JSON. A move takes the tracks on its pads along as a drag does; a locked footprint is refused with the reason.
