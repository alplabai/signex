- **PCB view controls: ratsnest toggle, Layers panel and active layer (#2223)** — View gains Toggle Ratsnest, Next Layer / Previous Layer, Toggle Layer Visibility (the active layer) and Show All Layers, greyed unless a PCB is open, and a Layers panel row. In a PCB tab `+` and `*` step to the next shown copper layer and `-` to the previous (Altium and Classic profiles); in an undocked PCB window they act on that window's board. The Layers panel lists the board's copper, technical and mechanical layers, each with an eye, a colour swatch and its name; a click on a copper row makes it the active layer. A hidden layer is not drawn and cannot be hovered, picked or boxed, and a pad goes with its copper layer. The active copper layer is drawn last and the other copper layers are dimmed; hiding the active layer makes the next shown copper layer active. A status-bar pill shows the active layer. The view is kept per open board for the session and is never written to the `.snxpcb`.
