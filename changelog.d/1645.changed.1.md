- **Shortcuts follow the OS (#1645)** — keymap profiles accept a platform modifier `Mod` (⌘ on macOS, Ctrl on Windows and Linux) and sections limited to one OS with `platform = "macos"`. On macOS, shortcut hints use Apple symbols (`⇧⌘S`, `⌃Q`), the recorder stores ⌘ as `Mod`, chords the system owns (⌘Q, ⌘H, ⌘M, ⌘Space, ⌘Tab) are flagged, and the Components preview canvases use ⌘ instead of Control. With Ctrl, ⌘ or Alt held, a key whose layout character matches no binding is retried at its US-layout position (Turkish-Q ⌘ö reaches `Mod+,`). The built-in profiles are unchanged, and Windows and Linux behave as before.
