- **Alternate parts per BOM line and a priced BOM from the command line (#2938)** — `bom_add_alternate`, `bom_remove_alternate` and `bom_set_alternate_order` keep a line's alternate parts, in order of preference, in the project file, and the BOM export gains an Alternates column. `query_bom` answers the project's BOM priced for a number of boards from the cached prices, with each line's alternates and, when its part has no price or too little stock, the alternate that would be bought instead.
