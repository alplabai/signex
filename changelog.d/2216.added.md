- **Polygon pours fill their copper (#2216)** — a new Polygon Pour tool (Place ▸ Polygon Pour, the PCB Active Bar, `P G`) draws a pour on the active copper layer on the net under its first corner. Its copper is computed: the outline less its holes, less other nets' pads, tracks, vias and solid zones grown by the clearance, less keepouts, and drawn as copper inside the stroked outline. Same-net pads join solidly (no thermal relief yet); the fill is recomputed when the board changes and not saved.
