- **Breaking for headless callers: wires and buses from operands are checked and answer with data (#2199)** — `place_wire`, `draw_wire`, `place_bus` and `draw_bus` run from `start` and `end` now refuse a run that touches something it was not drawn to, joins differently named nets or buses without `allow_merge=true`, crosses a symbol body, is not horizontal and vertical, or leaves an end unconnected, and the CLI reports `answered` with the run's report instead of `ran`; `apply_edits` includes each edit's answer in `results`. Pass `allow_open=true` for a deliberately open end. A click on the canvas is unchanged.
- **Breaking for headless callers: labels, power ports, junctions and no-connects from operands are checked for shorts and joins (#2199)** — `place_net_label`, `place_local_net_label`, `place_global_label`, `place_hierarchical_label` and `place_power_symbol` run from `at` (a coordinate or a pin reference) are refused when the net already at that point carries other names and not the new one, and `place_junction` is refused when it would join two nets with different names; pass `allow_merge=true` to merge on purpose. A label, power port, junction or no-connect placed by pin reference is also refused, with no escape, when it would pull another net onto the pin's (a wire passing under the tip, or another pin on it). A name the net already carries (its alias from a child sheet's hierarchical label included), a net that carries no name or only aliases, or an empty point is allowed; names are judged across the project: a net's own names are its net labels, global labels and power ports, its aliases the names behind a child's hierarchical label (a child's local labels on that net included), and two nets joined with different own names, with no own names and different aliases, or an own-named net carrying aliases joined to an alias-only net with other aliases need `allow_merge`, whatever the order of the nets, while the same names join freely (two placements of one child on the same entry share a bus) and so does a parent net with no alias wired to a child's entry. The check on one sheet follows the same rule, with hierarchical labels as aliases. A run's answer, a bus run's included, lists the aliases of the joined net in `aliases`. A no-connect and a power flag are not judged (decisions: https://github.com/alplabai/signex/issues/2199#issuecomment-6750784216 and https://github.com/alplabai/signex/issues/2199#issuecomment-6759237104). When the project cannot be read completely and the net reaches another sheet, the edit is refused with the reason unless `allow_merge=true`.
