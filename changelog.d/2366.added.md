- **Change a placed component's footprint from the board (#2366)** — the Footprint Name `…` button in the board Component view, and Change Footprint... in the board right-click menu, open the footprint picker with the component's own library footprints listed first. Picking one swaps the footprint of the selected components as one undo step: they keep their place, side, designator and value, pads keep their nets by pad number, and an old pad with no match on the new footprint is listed in Messages with its net. A locked component, or a footprint the board cannot hold, is refused with the reason. The Library row now shows the library's name.
