- **Graphite library-editor widgets (#114)** — the footprint editor gets a Layers panel grouped by board side with eye toggles and "Show all" / "Top side only"; the symbol editor gets a Pins table (select, rename and hide a pin through the existing pin edits); the sketch DOF chip uses the theme's `info`, `ok` and `err` tokens; the footprint canvas and layer swatches use the Graphite layer colours. Both panels are openable from the panel list. Legacy themes keep their layer colours and the plain DOF text.
