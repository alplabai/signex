- **Undoing or redoing a project-wide step checks the library again (#2476)** — an undo or redo of a step that moved several sheets or a board and its sheets together (Replace All, annotate, sheet numbering, Assign Footprints, a pin or gate swap, an agent's edit) now re-checks the embedded library symbols against the library, as a single undo already did, so a symbol the step brought back in an older version is flagged.
