- **Footprint role forms, menus and a live grid preview (#2523)** — every role sub-form in the footprint editor (sketch pad, pattern, pour, keepout, cutout) opens with a header and a line-art drawing; a keepout gets a Layer dropdown, a cutout a "Limit depth (pocket)" switch, and a sketch pad's paste and mask expansions are editable as Rule or Manual. The footprint pad menu gains Move Selection By…, Align ▸, Rotate Selection, Flip Selection, Rectangular Pattern… and Edit Sketch, and the symbol menu gains Rotate Selection and Align ▸ Align To Grid. Grid Properties (Ctrl+G) draws the fine and coarse grid live with a pad for scale; the step and the coarse spacing can be typed on the drawing, and text there that is not a length is outlined red and holds OK until it is fixed or Esc closes it.
