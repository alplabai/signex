- **Reference dimensions and the mockup sketch look (#2518)** — a sketch dimension can be converted to a reference from its menu or its Properties conflict row: it measures the geometry, draws dashed in purple with its live value and never drives the solve, and Make driving turns it back (one undo step each; one that over-constrains is refused). On the board ground the sketch now draws green when fully constrained, blue under, red over, with the constraint marks in small pills (a dot for coincident) and values on dimension lines; mirrored patterns and patterns of patterns show their glyphs (`⇔ L×4`, `P×4 of L×4`).
