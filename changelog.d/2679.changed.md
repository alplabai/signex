- **Notebook badges replace the annotation flags (#2679, #2795)** — a part the Design Notebook writes about now carries one badge with its block count, coloured by the worst status of those blocks (blue for notes and calculations, green when every measurement passes, amber for an open issue), instead of one flag per kind; a net carries its badge on its labels. Hovering a badge lists its blocks, clicking it opens the notebook at the first one, and View ▸ Notebook Badges (B) hides or shows them; they are on by default.
