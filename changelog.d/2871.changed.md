- **Pour Cutout places a cutout region of its own (#2871)** — Place ▸ Pour Cutout no longer cuts the drawn area out of the pour's outline. It places a cutout region on the active copper layer, saved with the board, that every polygon pour on that layer leaves empty when it fills. Click inside the region to select it: it moves by dragging, its layer changes in Properties (which also shows its vertices and area), and it deletes like any object, each one undo step. Moving or deleting it and repouring brings the copper back. The pour's outline is never changed. DRC, Gerber, PDF and the 3D view read the filled copper as before. Slice Pour is unchanged. A board without a cutout opens and saves as before.
