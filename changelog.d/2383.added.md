- **Board Selection Filter presets, and Keepouts, Regions and Fills can be picked (#2383)** — the PCB Active Bar's Selection Filter is now the same dropdown as the schematic's (All / None, presets, a chip per kind). Save the kinds that are on as a named preset from the board Properties, apply it with one click there or in the dropdown, rename, update or delete it; presets are kept across restarts. Keepouts, solid regions and fills drawn on the board are picked on their outline or inside them, and their filter chips turn that on and off.
