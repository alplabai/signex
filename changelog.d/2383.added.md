- **Board Selection Filter presets, and Keepouts, Regions and Fills can be picked (#2383)** — save the kinds that are on as a named preset, apply it with one click from the board Properties or the PCB Active Bar's Selection Filter, rename, update or delete it; presets are kept across restarts. Keepouts, solid regions and fills drawn on the board are now picked on their outline or inside them, and their filter chips turn that on and off.
