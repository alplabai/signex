- **Track, via and pad Properties on a board (#2355)** — selecting tracks, vias or pads shows their own Properties panel: Net Information (the net and class as links that select their copper, the routed length of the selection and of the whole net, Show More), and the kind's fields — a track's location, net (with a pick button that takes the net from the board), copper layer, width, start, end and length; a via's net, span, position, diameter and hole size; a pad's designator, net, position, rotation, size, shape and hole size. Several selected items show the values they share and leave the rest blank. Each edit is one undo step. Fields the board cannot store yet (per-item mask expansion, locks on tracks and vias, templates, testpoints, via types, hole tolerances, per-layer stacks, electrical type, delay) are shown disabled with a tooltip.
