- **Layer tab menu, and tracks and ratsnest that follow a moved component (#2379)** — Right-clicking a layer tab under the board opens a menu with Hide this layer, Show only this layer (that layer becomes the active one; every other layer is hidden, the board outline and the Multi-Layer row stay as they were) and Show all layers. Dragging a component, or moving it with Edit ▸ Move, now takes the tracks connected to its pads along: the track end on a pad moves with the pad and the other end stays, so the track stays connected at both ends; the move and the track edits are one undo step. While the component is carried, its connected tracks and the ratsnest lines to its pads are drawn where they will be, instead of the ratsnest lines disappearing until the drop.
