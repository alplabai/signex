- **DRC waivers in the project (#2307)** — a project can accept single DRC findings with a reason and an author in `rules.drc_waivers` of the `.snxprj`, stored like ERC waivers. A waiver matches a finding by its rule and the items it names, and lapses when one of those items is moved, resized, moved to another layer or net, so the finding shows again. Two findings that name the same rule and items cannot be waived. `check_drc` lists waived findings under `waived` and lapsed waivers under `lapsed`. Waivers never change the rule-set hash, and a project without them is saved exactly as before. Run DRC and the DRC panel do not use them yet.
