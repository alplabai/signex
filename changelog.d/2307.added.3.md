- **DRC waivers lapse when their objects change (#2307)** — a waived DRC finding comes back, and Messages says so, once one of its tracks, vias, pads or other objects is moved, resized or put on another layer or net, and `check_drc` now lists the board's waived findings under `waived` and lapsed waivers under `lapsed`, with each finding's `evidence.waiver_key` the id `waive_drc_violation` takes.
