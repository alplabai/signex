- **DXF import onto a board (#2410)** — File ▸ Import ▸ DXF to Current Layer... puts a DXF drawing's lines, arcs, circles and polylines on the active layer of the board in view, and DXF as Board Outline... makes its largest closed shape the board outline. The file's origin is the board origin and its units come from the file. Each import is one undo step, and the same commands (`import_dxf`, `import_dxf_outline`) take `path`, `layer` and `document` operands from the CLI or an agent. Design notes for panelisation, pin and gate swap and design reuse blocks are in `docs/BOARD_IMPORT_AND_REUSE.md`.
