- **Saving a symbol or footprint now also updates the rows of your other open libraries that use it (#2004)** — a row in another library that binds the primitive moves to its new version, under that library's own workflow rules (a released row of a Team library keeps its old pin), so parts placed from it are flagged as out of date. A row update a save could not finish is also kept for that file after its editor is closed and is carried out by the next save of it or by reopening the editor; it is dropped when the app quits, and Quit names the files that still owe rows. A library that is briefly unreadable during a save keeps its rows owed instead of being skipped.
