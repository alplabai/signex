- **Tabs that do not fit go behind a "+N" menu (#2262)** — document tabs no longer shrink below their name or scroll. The tabs that do not fit hide behind a "+N" button at the end of the strip; its menu lists them with their kind, and picking one brings it to the front. The active tab always shows. While a floating panel is dragged, every dock it can go into is outlined and named, and the one it would land in is filled. A panel in its own window draws a title strip with the active document's name, Dock back and Close; both put the panel back in its home dock.
