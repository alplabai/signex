- **Select on the PCB canvas (#2191)** — hovering the board outlines the pad, track or via under the pointer, a click selects it, Shift adds and Ctrl or Cmd toggles, and dragging a box selects what lies inside it (left to right) or touches it (right to left). Esc clears the selection and a box in progress. Items whose coordinates cannot be read are listed in the Messages panel. A left press on the board no longer reaches the app-wide handler that closes an open context menu.
