- **Board Properties panel (#2353)** — With nothing selected on a board, Properties now shows the whole board: a search field that filters the rows, General and Health Check tabs, and the sections Selection Filter, Snap Options, Board Information, Grid Manager, Guide Manager and Other. The Selection Filter chips decide what a click, a box and the hover pick (Components, Tracks, Pads, Vias), and the header sums up the chips that are on. Snap Options turns grid snapping on or off, sets Snapping to All Layers, Current Layer or Off (Shift+E steps through them on a board), picks the object points a moved footprint snaps to (Pad Centers and Via Centers by default; also track vertices, polygon vertices, board shape, footprint origins and texts) and the snap distance. Board Information counts the board's size, area, density, components, layers, nets and primitives; the component counts select those components and the DRC count opens the results. The Grid Manager edits the board's snap grid step as one undo step and turns the visible grid on or off; Units switches between mm and mils. Settings the board file cannot hold yet (more grids, guides, polygon naming, designator display, sheet settings, parameters, board reports) are shown greyed out with a tooltip that says why.
