- **Part bookmarks follow "One bookmark per part" (#1298)** — in the Export PDF dialog, part bookmarks are now switched by **One bookmark per part** under Bookmarks (on by default). **Include Component Parameters** is gone from Bookmarks and Nets: it used to switch the part bookmarks, and the exporter writes no parameter rows yet, so it would change nothing. A PDF with part and net bookmarks both off still gets one bookmark per sheet unless **One bookmark per sheet** is cleared (before, it had no bookmarks).
