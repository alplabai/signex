- **Routing corpus of 50 real boards (#2481)** — `testdata/routing-corpus/` holds 50 third-party boards (dense BGA and DDR, multi-layer, RF, power, MCU and keyboard boards) under their own licences, listed in `MANIFEST.tsv` with source, pinned commit, licence and attribution. The routing soak and bench run on them; a CI gate (`scripts/check_routing_corpus.sh`) keeps every board in the manifest and every licence on the allow-list. The boards are stored in Git LFS.
