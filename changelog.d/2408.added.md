- **Via stitching, teardrops and gloss (#2408)** — Tools ▸ Via Stitching fills the selected copper area, or every pour, with vias of its net on a grid, clear of other nets and keepouts; Tools ▸ Add Teardrops and Remove Teardrops add or remove tapered copper where tracks enter pads and vias of their net; Tools ▸ Gloss Selected Routes cuts the free corners of the selected routes where the straight track keeps the clearance. Each is one undo step, keeps the clearance the design rule check asks for, and runs from an agent or the CLI with the board and items named.
