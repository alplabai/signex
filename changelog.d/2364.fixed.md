- **Placing a component on a board places its footprint (#2364)** — on a board tab, Place from the Components panel (row menu, picker, or a row dragged onto the board) now puts the chosen footprint (the Footprint dropdown's pick, or the default) on the cursor, drawn as the board draws it and dimmed, on the board grid. Space turns it, Y or Flip Selection moves it to the other side, and each click places a copy as one undo step with the next free designator (D1, D2, …). Esc or a right-click stops. A component with no footprint, or one the board cannot hold, is refused with the reason in Messages instead of showing a bare name tag.
