- **Variant Manager tab (#2849)** — variants open in a document tab of their own, `<project> — Variants`, from the Project tab's Variants row or the project tree; opening it again brings it to the front. Click a cell of the parts matrix to edit that part in that variant on the right: fit it or leave it out, give it its own value, footprint or field (with a reset back to Base), each one undo step. Duplicate copies a variant with what it fits, and Set as default marks the default variant with a star and a pill. The BOM lists a variant's parts with these settings. They are saved in the `.snxprj`. Settings, Compare variants and Open in BOM Studio are shown greyed: not built yet.
