- **PDF watermark, PDF layers and bookmark choices (#1298)** — the schematic PDF exporter can print a DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or custom watermark (opacity, angle, colour, all pages or the first only, an optional revision line), write PDF layers a reader can switch (wires and buses, parts, net labels and ports, power ports, title block and border, watermark; the watermark always prints), and choose sheet and part bookmarks, with parts listed by sheet or A to Z in natural designator order. All are off or unchanged by default, so existing exports stay the same.
