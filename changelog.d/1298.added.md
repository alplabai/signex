- **PDF watermark, PDF layers and bookmark choices (#1298)** — the schematic PDF exporter can print a DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or custom watermark (opacity, angle, all pages or the first only, an optional "Rev · date" line in English; the Export PDF dialog prints it in a fixed red), write PDF layers a reader can switch (wires and buses, parts, net labels and ports, power ports, title block and border, watermark; the watermark always prints), and choose sheet and part bookmarks, with parts listed by sheet or A to Z in natural designator order. The watermark and the PDF layers are off by default, in the exporter and in the Export PDF dialog, so a default export prints no watermark and stays a flat PDF.
