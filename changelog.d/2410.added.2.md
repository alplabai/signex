- **Panelisation (#2410)** — Tools ▸ Panelize Board arrays the board into a manufacturing panel: copies with gaps, rails, fiducials, tooling holes, and mouse bites or V-score lines. The Panel section of the board's Properties edits every setting, the board view previews the panel, and Fabrication Outputs writes the whole panel as one board. Each edit is one undo step; agents and the CLI drive it with `panelize_board`, `set_board_panel`, `remove_board_panel` and `board_panel`.
