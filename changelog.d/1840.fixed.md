- **Saving a symbol no longer makes an open, edited Component Preview unsaveable (#1840)** — a preview with unsaved edits of a row the symbol save bumped keeps its edits and takes the bumped pins and revision, including when Save All or Quit ▸ Save All saves the symbol and the row together. A row that anything other than that symbol save also changed is still refused as changed by someone else, and its edit is kept.
