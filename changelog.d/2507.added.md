- **Dimension kinds on the board (#2507)** — Place ▸ Dimension and the PCB Active Bar's Dimensions dropdown offer Aligned (measured along its two points, the dimension boards had before), Linear Horizontal and Linear Vertical (measured across or up), and Radial (one click on an arc or circle; the value reads `R` and the radius). Each kind uses the dimension look settings. A radial dimension follows its arc or circle as it moves or changes size, in the same undo step. A board with only aligned dimensions saves as before; a board with another kind saves as `snxpcb/1.11`, which older builds open read-only.
