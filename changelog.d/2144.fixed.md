- **Moving a child-sheet pin no longer refuses a correct rubber-band (#2144)** — the move check looks up the net under a child-sheet pin's old position without changing the connectivity it reads. Before, an old position in the middle of a wire with no dot that also held a no-connect pin tip or a power-port tip merged two nets in the check, so a correct rubber-band could be turned into a plain move with a warning. The saved design was not affected.
