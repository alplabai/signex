- **Block diagram Power tree page and export pages (#2796, #1313)** — the Power tree reads the variant picked in the toolbar's Variant field, names its scenario, sources and tightest rail on a totals line, colours load bars by their share of the rail, and opens a part's sheet when the part is clicked. Show GND hides ground connections until ticked. The PDF export prints the pages picked (block diagram, power tree, budget table) on A4, A3, A2, Letter or Tabloid, landscape or portrait, also as `export_block_diagram` operands. The Projects tree lists every block diagram in a project's folder, and the installers associate `.snxblk` files with Signex.
