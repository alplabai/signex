- **Document tab menu: Close Tabs to the Right, Copy Path, Reveal in Projects (#2262)** — right-click a document tab to close the tabs after it on the strip (a tab open in its own window stays open), copy the file's path, or select the file in the Projects panel. The menu rows carry icons and the Close row shows its shortcut. The dock tab menu now puts Undock / Float first and Close last, after a separator.
