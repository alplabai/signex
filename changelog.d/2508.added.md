- **Orientation tile and Home View (#2508)** — a small tile above the canvas zoom pill shows where +X and +Y point: the arrows turn with View Rotation, Flip Board View mirrors +X, and a caption reads the turn ("90°", "Bottom", "Bottom · 90°"). A Home button left of the zoom pill, View ▸ Home View and the `home_view` command (no default key) set the rotation to 0°, turn Flip off and fit the board; the button carries a dot while the view is turned or flipped. The board canvas gets the zoom pill too, the Layers panel's View Options gains an Orientation eye that hides the tile, and the schematic canvas shows the same tile and Home button.
