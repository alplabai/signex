- **Pinned Projects on the Start screen (#2305)** — Pin a project from its Recent Projects row (the Pin button on hover or the right-click menu) to keep it in a Pinned Projects section above the recent list, in the order you pinned it, with no limit; Unpin takes it off, and Recent hides when every recent project is pinned. Pins are saved with your preferences. A pinned project missing at start-up, or found missing when you click it, is marked "Missing on disk" with a Remove button.
