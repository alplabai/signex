- **Pinned Projects on the Start screen (#2305)** — Pin a project from its Recent Projects row (the Pin button on hover or the right-click menu) to keep it in a Pinned Projects section above the recent list, in the order you pinned it, with no limit; Unpin takes it off, and Recent hides when every recent project is pinned. Pins are saved with your preferences. Pins whose files are gone are found in the background at start-up, or when you click one, and are marked "Missing on disk" with a Remove button; a click on a missing pin also writes a warning to Messages.
