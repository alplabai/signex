- **Key names follow the language (#2314)** — modifier and key names in shortcut labels (Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Win, Super, DEL, HOME and the rest) come from the message catalog, so menus, the native menu, the command palette, the shortcuts sheet, context-menu hints and Preferences rename them on a language switch without a restart. Preferences search still finds a key by its English name, and profile files keep the English spellings.
