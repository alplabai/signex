- **Document Options, redrawn (#2531)** — with nothing selected on a sheet, Properties shows the sheet's file name beside **Document Options** and switches General / Parameters with a segmented control. **General** opens with a line-art **Grid and snap** drawing (grid dots, snap ticks with a snapped cursor, the pin-end hotspot ring; the two pitches are labelled after their fields) and lists one setting per row: Units as mm / mils, **Visible Grid** and **Snap Grid** with their on/off box first (the pitch greys out and takes no input while off, and that grid leaves the drawing), Snap to Hotspots, Canvas Font and Sheet Color. **Page Options** opens with a **Sheet** drawing (the paper at its orientation, the border with its zone letters and numbers, the title block and the origin), then Format, Orientation and Origin as segmented switches; the Paper list names each size with its millimetres (*A4 · 297 × 210 mm*) instead of a separate Width / Height line, and Format = Custom swaps Paper and Orientation for one **Size** row of typed W / H. Zone counts are typed numbers (V / H on one row, Enter applies) under a **Show Border** switch and grey out with it. Hovering a field highlights its part of the drawing and the other way round.
- **Parameters as one grouped list (#2531)** — the Parameters tab has a filter field and **All / Parameters / Rules** chips with counts (Rules shows an empty card: a sheet has no rules yet). Rows sit in three folding groups: **Title block**, **Sheet variables** (each with its result in a pill, an inline error such as *Error: unknown variable RSNS*, a remove button and **Add variable** at the end) and **Built-in** (read-only). A title-block row whose value comes from the project shows a **project** pill instead of "(project)" / "(overrides project)" in its name; typing a value overrides it for this sheet, as one undo step. An unset field reads *Not set* in muted italic.
