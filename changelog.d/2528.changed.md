- **Net colour palette, custom picker and Move Selection follow the Graphite design (#2528)** — the F5 palette is a 360 px window with a filter, one aligned swatch column (hatched for no colour) and net names in mono, coloured nets first; clicking a row opens a chooser with ten colours, Custom… and Clear, and a palette colour now colours the net's wires on the sheet as one undo step. "Clear all colours" clears the active sheet's net colours as one undo step. The custom picker shows 48 swatches in 8 columns with an accent ring on the pick, an Old / New preview and an editable Hex field kept in step with R / G / B. Move Selection is a 360 px dialog with the count as the subtitle ("3 items selected"), ΔX / ΔY side by side with the display unit as a suffix (offsets are read in that unit), a read-only preview of the move, a grid-step help line, Enter to move and a Move button.
