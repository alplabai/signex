- **Recover unsaved edits after a crash (#1164)** — when you open a schematic that has an autosave recovery copy newer than the saved file, Signex asks whether to Restore it, Discard it or Decide later (Esc). Restore loads the copy as unsaved edits and writes nothing to your file until you save; a copy that is older than the file, or identical to it, is removed without asking. Quitting now waits for a recovery copy that is still being written, so a copy of edits you discarded no longer survives the quit. Annotate and Reset Duplicate Designators remove the recovery copy of a sheet file they rewrite. Only schematics are autosaved; symbol, footprint and PCB editors are not covered yet.
