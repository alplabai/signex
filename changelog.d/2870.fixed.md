- **Every sheet object moves by dragging (#2870)** — parts, power ports, wires, buses, bus entries, labels, sheet symbols and their entries, junctions, no-connects, No ERC markers, harness connectors, text, graphics, dimensions, tables and directives are now each checked to move on press, drag and release, snapped and with their wires kept as a Move keeps them, as one undo step.
- **A harness entry slides along its connector (#2870)** — dragging or moving a harness entry used to do nothing; it now slides to the nearest slot on its connector's side, the other entries closing up, and its signal wire stays on it. One Undo puts it back.
