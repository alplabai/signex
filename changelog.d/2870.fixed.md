- **Every sheet object moves by dragging (#2870)** — parts, power ports, wires, buses, bus entries, labels, sheet symbols and their entries, junctions, no-connects, No ERC markers, harness connectors, text, graphics, dimensions, tables and directives are now each checked to move on press, drag and release, snapped and with their wires kept as a Move keeps them, as one undo step.
- **Every board object drags (#2870)** — tracks, arcs, fills, solid regions, polygon pours, keepouts, strings, dimensions, lines, circles, rectangles, text frames and rooms now move when you press on one and drag it, as footprints and vias already did. The drop snaps to the grid and is one undo step. A room takes its parts along. Press anywhere inside a pour, fill, solid region, keepout or room to drag it; a track, pad, via or part on top of it is dragged instead, and a box selection starts only on empty board.
