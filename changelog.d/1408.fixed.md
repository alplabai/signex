- **Power ports placed from the Active Bar now carry a connection pin (#1408)** — each style is placed with a library entry whose pin sits at the anchor and whose head is the #1407 body, so the netlist, ERC and the SVG export read the same body the canvas draws. The entry is keyed by the port's `lib_id`, so the Active Bar style survives, and a Properties-panel style change adds the new style's entry in the same undo step. Sheets whose power ports have no entry still load and draw their built-in body.
