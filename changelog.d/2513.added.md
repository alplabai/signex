- **A dragged track keeps its connections (#2513)** — press on a track or via, selected or not, and drag to move it in one go. The tracks joined to a dragged track's ends now stretch to stay joined, with their far ends kept on their pads and vias. A stretched track stays at 0°, 45° or 90° as a routed one does, bending at one 45° corner when it has to; the tracks a dragged via or part takes along bend the same way, and a Command Log Replay of the move bends them as the drop did. The preview shows them where the drop leaves them. One undo puts it all back. A locked track or via stays where it is and Messages says it is locked. Hold Ctrl to move the track alone.
