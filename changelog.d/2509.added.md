- **Board Place keys in the Classic shortcut profile (#2509)** — on a board, `Shift+T` places a track, `Shift+V` a via, `Shift+G` a polygon pour, `Shift+Alt+F` a fill and `Shift+Alt+R` a solid region (⇧T, ⇧V, ⇧G, ⌥⇧F and ⌥⇧R on macOS). They are live on boards only and leave every existing Classic key as it was.
