- **Select > Connection follows the real net (#2106)** — it now picks the same wires, junctions and labels the netlist puts on the net, and the Active Bar uses the selected item itself as the start. Behaviour changes:
  - It follows power-port merges: selecting one GND wire selects every GND-connected wire, junction and label on the sheet.
  - It follows T-junctions and same-name labels.
  - A net seed no longer pulls in a touching bus, and a bus seed no longer reaches a wire (ADR-0002 D5.4).
  - A bus seed expands along the bus run the bus analysis builds: segments joined end to end, at a T or through a junction. It also selects the labels that touch the run: a bus-name label anywhere along it, any other label only at a segment end, so a net label such as `SDA` mid-span on a bus is not part of the bus.
  - A label the bus analysis attaches to a bus, and a junction on a bus with no wire, select that bus run. Before, a junction on a bus-only T selected those buses by endpoint walk, and a label on a bus selected only itself.
  - A wire seed now picks up labels that sit mid-span on the net's wires, not only at wire ends.
