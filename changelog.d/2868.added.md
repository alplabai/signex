- **Board dimension settings (#2868)** — a board dimension's Properties gain Arrows (Filled, Open or None, with length and width), Text (Mono or Sans font, bold, italic, text width, and the value above the line, inline in a break of the line, or at a point you set, with a gap), Extension Lines (offset from the measured point, overshoot past the line, width), and Units and Format (mm, mil or in, the unit shown or not, prefix and suffix text, trailing zeros). Each change is one undo step and is saved with the board. New dimensions start from Preferences ▸ PCB ▸ PCB Defaults. Boards saved before keep their look.
