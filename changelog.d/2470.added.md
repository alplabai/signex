- **Update PCB from schematic from the command line (#2470)** — `update_pcb_from_schematic document=<project, sheet or board> dry_run=true` lists every footprint to add, remove, change or swap, every designator, value, net and pad net change as JSON without changing anything; `apply=true` makes those changes as one undo step and answers with the same list of what was done. It runs headless, through `signex-cli run` and through a running Signex.
