- **The command palette opens in the title-bar search field (#2262)** — while the palette is open the search field is its input, with a focus border and an Esc hint, and the results card opens right under it; a click on the field keeps the palette open. For a typed query the result groups are ordered by their best match, and the footer counts the results. The field is up to 400 px wide; in a window too narrow for it, a search button takes its place.
