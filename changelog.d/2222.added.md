- **PCB design-rule check (#2222)** — `run_drc` (and a Run DRC button in the Messages panel) checks the board for copper of different nets closer than the minimum clearance, copper of different nets that touches, and nets the ratsnest still shows open. The minimum is the board setup's clearance, 0.2 mm when it holds none, raised per net by its net class. When the default is used, the Messages panel and log say so. Violations are listed in the Messages panel; clicking one selects its items on the board. The check only reads the board. Copper with no net, hole-to-hole, silk, courtyard and zone-fill clearance are not checked yet.
