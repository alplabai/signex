- **ERC and the netlist now agree on power ports (#675)** — same-value power ports on separate wires join into one net (no more `GND` plus `GND_2`), a port joins same-name Global/Power labels, and ERC names the net the same way the netlist does. Pins of two different parts touching tip to tip stay unconnected, as before, but ERC now warns "pins touch but are not connected — add a wire" naming both pins instead of leaving it silent.
