- **Signal harnesses** — Place > Ports & Connectors > Signal Harness, Harness Connector and Harness Entry (and the Active Bar's Harness group) now place real objects: thick harness wires, connectors with entries on their flat side, and entries added by a click. A sheet entry can carry a harness (Properties > Harness type). Same-named entries of one harness are one net, called `SPI_BUS.SCK` unless a label names it; nested harnesses compile to `DEBUG.UART.TX`; a harness joins the child sheet through its harness port. Two new ERC warnings, harness entry mismatch and harness type conflict; the connector's Properties show a mismatch with Rename and Go to buttons, and the project's harness types are edited in a harness definitions view (`open_harness_definitions`). PDF and SVG exports print harnesses. See `docs/SIGNAL_HARNESSES.md`.
