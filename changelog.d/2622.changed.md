- **Board 3D view toolbar (#2622)** — the bar at the foot of the 3D view becomes one floating toolbar at the top centre: a 2D | 3D switch, the viewpoints Top, Iso, Front, Left, Right and Back, Reset camera and Fit, Flip, which turns the board over in one animated step, Explode, which moves the core, copper, mask and parts apart, a lighting preset (Studio, Workshop, Outdoor, Dark) whose backdrop is the same in both themes, and a screenshot button that copies the view to the clipboard, with Save PNG… in its menu. A view cube sits at the top right and a status pill names the view, the board size and the number of bodies. Bottom is no longer a button (use Flip), and Angled is now Iso. Nothing in the toolbar changes the board, the undo history or the saved file.
