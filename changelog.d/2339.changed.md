- **Requirement cards (#2339)** — Run checks moves to the Requirements header with a summary row of the last run's counts; each card's header holds the Enabled switch, the read-only id, a one-line summary and the outcome pill, and a passed card starts closed. A requirement switched off reads Off and is left out of the counts. A requirement with no id now gets its Passed or Failed verdict from its place in the list instead of always reading Not checked.
