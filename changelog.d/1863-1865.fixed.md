- **A board changed on disk is no longer overwritten by a save (#1863)** — PCB saves are compared like sheet saves and offer the same Reload, Overwrite and Cancel card. The baseline after a save is the bytes that were written, and a Reload, History restore or open no longer waits on a running write.
- **Reload keeps edits made while it reads, and a gone folder says so (#1864)** — a Reload whose document changed during the read keeps the edits and asks again; when the folder itself is gone the card says so and offers no Re-create.
- **Symlinks and closed tabs keep the disk records right (#1865)** — History restore and the project-file entry patch write through a symlink, a renamed or Save As sheet is still found through a link to it, and closing a tab forgets its baseline.
