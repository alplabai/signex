- **Board sketch bake targets Slot, Pour, Mechanical layer and Component anchor** — the board role picker now lists Board outline, Cutout, Slot, Keepout, Pour, V-score, Mechanical layer, Component anchor and Mounting hole. A Slot is a cutout whose profile must be two parallel lines joined by two tangent arcs; it bakes routed at its own radius, and any other profile is refused by name. A Pour bakes a copper pour zone on the picked layer and net, a Mechanical layer drawing a board drawing on the picked mechanical layer, and a Component anchor moves the picked component's origin to the point (a locked component stays put, and Messages says so). All of it is one undo step with the outline, and the bake preview draws and counts it. A board whose sketch uses these targets saves as `snxpcb/1.7`, which older builds open read-only; any other board keeps its token.
