- **Workspace layouts (#2678)** — View ▸ Window ▸ Layouts arranges the panels in one step: four built-ins (Schematic, Layout, Review, Notebook) and layouts you save yourself with Save Layout…. Manage Layouts… renames and deletes your own. Update from current keeps your edit of a built-in in place of it, and Reset restores the built-in. Layouts are kept with your preferences, not in a project, and only change when you pick one; open documents are left as they are.
