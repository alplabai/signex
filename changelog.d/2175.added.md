- **Board pads take their nets from the schematic (#2175)** — net assignment fills the board's net table and each pad's net from the schematic netlist. A footprint links to its schematic symbol by sheet path and symbol UUID, never the designator (saved in an optional `sch_link` footprint column); a board made before the link is matched by designator for now and each such footprint is reported. It reports pins with no pad, pads on two nets and footprints that share a link. Applying it returns the edit that undoes it. A pad's net can be locked (saved in an optional `net_locked` pad column) and unlocked: a locked pad keeps its net number, a rename of that number follows, and the sync otherwise leaves it alone and reports it. A pad with no pad number, or one that matches no pin of its linked symbol, locks itself, so the lock persists.
