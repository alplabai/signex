- **Board pads take their nets from the schematic (#2175)** — net assignment fills the board's net table and each pad's net from the schematic netlist, linking a pad to a pin by designator and pad number for now, and reports pins with no pad, pads on two nets and designators shared by two footprints. Applying it returns the edit that undoes it. A pad's net can be locked (saved in an optional `net_locked` pad column): the sync never changes a locked pad and reports it, and a pad the schematic cannot speak for locks itself, so the lock persists.
