- **Board pads take their nets from the schematic (#2175)** — net assignment fills the board's net table and each pad's net from the schematic netlist. A footprint links to its schematic part by sheet path and the UUIDs of all the part's units (so a multi-unit part works), never the designator; the link is saved in an optional `sch_link` footprint column. A footprint with no link is reported and left untouched; a separate command, link footprints by designator, links an older board once, only where one part matches, and undoes exactly. Sync reports pins with no pad, pads on two nets and footprints that share a link. Applying it returns the edit that undoes it. A pad's net can be locked (saved in an optional `net_locked` pad column) and unlocked: a locked pad keeps its net number, a rename of that number follows, and the sync otherwise leaves it alone and reports it. On a footprint that is in the schematic, a pad with no pad number or no matching pin locks itself, so the lock persists; a footprint the schematic lacks is reported and its pads are not locked.
