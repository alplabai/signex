- **Selection Filter presets are edited in place (#2873)** — a click on a preset applies it and selects it ("Editing <name>"); each chip click then changes that preset at once, until All - On, None or a second click on it. Save Chips is gone; the name field and Delete act on the selected preset, and Add Preset still saves the chips that are on.
