- **Differential pairs, Quick Route, Multi-Route and pair length tuning (#2389)** — the PCB Active Bar's Route group adds Differential Pair Routing (click copper of a `_P`/`_N` or `+`/`-` net, or of a net class with a pair gap: both tracks run side by side at the class's coupled width and gap, never closer than the rules allow, and walk around other nets together), Quick Route (straight to the clicked point at the rule width, other nets not avoided) and Multi-Route (the selected nets run side by side as a bus). A new Tune group runs Length Tuning (the selected tracks' nets lengthened to the longest of them) and Differential Pair Length Tuning (the shorter net of a pair lengthened to match the other). Each route and each tuning is one undo step; all are in Place, the command palette and the agent channel (the two tuning commands take `document`, `net` and `value`).
