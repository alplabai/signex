- **One Layers panel for boards and footprints (#2359)** — the Panels list had two "Layers" entries, and the footprint one said "Open a `.snxfpt`" on a board tab. There is one Layers panel now, following the active tab: a board's layers on a board tab, a footprint's layers on a footprint tab. A saved layout's footprint Layers panel opens as this one. On a board the layers are grouped as Signal, Plane, Mechanical, Overlay, Mask and Drill (the Multi-Layer row with the drill layers); each group has a Show all / Hide all button, any row's name makes that layer active, as its tab does, and a Single layer button matches Shift+S. The eye tooltips use the floating-card look and sit above the eye, so they no longer cover the row.
