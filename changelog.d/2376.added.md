- **Zoom presets on the canvas zoom pill** — clicking the zoom percentage at the bottom right of a schematic opens a small menu above it: 50 %, 100 %, 200 % and 400 % (the current level checked) zoom about the middle of the view, and Fit All and Fit Selection fit the view (Fit Selection is greyed with nothing selected). Hovering the pill's buttons names each action and its key in the active shortcut profile, and a thin line now sets Fit apart from the zoom buttons.
