- **Library editor reads no longer stall the window (#859)** — reloading a component row, the first visit of a Preview or Simulation tab, the Properties panel's symbol and footprint summaries of the selected row, and the reload after a primitive save's cascade now read on a background job and apply when it lands; a read that a newer one replaced, or whose library was closed or reopened, is dropped, and a reload never replaces edits made after it was asked for (a reload the user asked for says so in the Messages panel when it is refused). The panel shows "Reading symbol…" until its answer arrives, and a failed History-restore reload now says when the library could not be reopened.
