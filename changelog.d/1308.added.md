- **Compiled Design panel (#1308)** — View ▸ Compiled Design opens a panel beside Projects that shows the project as it compiles: the sheet hierarchy with repeated sheets expanded into their channels, parts and nets per sheet, and a Nets mode with the sheets each net crosses. `compile_project` (palette, or the panel's Recompile) builds it off the UI thread; nothing is written to disk. An edit to a compiled sheet marks its rows out of date; recompiling is never automatic.
