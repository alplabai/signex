- **Not-fitted parts are crossed out (#1294)** — a part marked DNP, or not fitted in the active variant, draws greyed with a cross over its body on the schematic canvas, and in PDF, SVG and print for the printed variant. Its designator and value stay readable. A part reads as the BOM reads it: a multi-unit part whose units sit on several sheets is crossed out on each of them, and a repeated sheet crosses a part out only when every channel leaves it out. Turn it off with Preferences ▸ Schematic ▸ Display ▸ Cross out not-fitted parts (on by default).
