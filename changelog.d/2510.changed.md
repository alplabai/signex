- **Symbol bodies are drawn filled** — the closed rectangles, polygons and circles of a symbol's body that the symbol leaves unfilled now draw with a soft theme tint on the canvas, in the symbol previews and in PDF, SVG and print. Preferences ▸ Schematic ▸ Display turns it off or picks another colour; a body the symbol fills itself keeps its own fill, and pins, text, open lines and power ports are never filled.
