- **Multi-channel sheets (#1279)** — a sheet symbol whose designator reads `Repeat(CH, 1, 4)` places its sheet once and compiles to four channels: every part takes a channel designator (`CH1_R5`), local nets a channel name (`VOUT_3V3_CH1`), plain sheet entries are shared and a `Repeat(VOUT_3V3)` entry joins each channel to its member of a bus on the parent. The netlist, BOM and PDF see every channel part. The symbol draws as a stack with a ×N pill on the canvas and in PDF and SVG output. Properties of a sheet symbol has a Repeat section (switch, channel name, from / to), the project's channel naming formats with a live preview, and its entries; its Name row now reads Designator. A repeated sheet gets channel tabs under its canvas; a channel tab is a read-only compiled view. New ERC rule `repeat_entry_needs_bus`. No file field is added: an older build reads a plain sheet symbol.
