- **Board view configuration in the Layers panel (#2363)** — the Layers panel has two tabs and a search field. Layers & Colors lists the copper layers with their number and type, the component layers as Top | Layer | Bottom pairs with an eye and a colour chip per side, the other technical layers, the other layers and the system colours; a chip opens the colours, and the picked colour applies to this board view on the canvas, the chip and the board texts. All Layers, Used On, Layer Sets, Active Layer and View From Bottom Side sit above the list. View Options shows or hides each kind of object, sets its transparency, and switches 3D, single-layer mode and the grid. Controls the board view cannot honour yet are greyed with a tooltip and tracked in #2480. Agents and the CLI reach every control through `set_layer_visible`, `set_active_layer`, `set_layer_color`, `show_used_layers_only`, `set_object_visible` and `set_object_transparency`.
