- **Placed parts follow their library row (#1867)** — placing a row stamps its Manufacturer and MPN, so ERC no longer reports `PartWithoutMpn`. A part whose row now has a different footprint, manufacturer or MPN is flagged as out of date, and Properties > "Update from library" brings it in line (with the change listed in Messages) in the same single undo step as the symbol copy. Nothing is rewritten without that click, and a footprint, Manufacturer or MPN you set on one part is kept while its row has not changed since the part was placed.
