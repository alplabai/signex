- **Document panes (#1307)** — drag a document tab onto an edge of the document area to move it into a new pane there, or hold Ctrl (Cmd on macOS) to open a second view of it. View ▸ Window has Tile Grid, Split Right, Split Down and Open Second View, and hovering a tile row previews which documents land in which pane. Each pane has its own tab strip and a ⋯ menu with Move Tab to Other Pane, Close Pane, Sync Zoom with Other Pane and Equal Pane Sizes; the splitters between panes drag, and a double click makes them equal. Each pane keeps its own pan and zoom, the panels follow the focused pane, and a part selected in one pane is outlined in the board or sheet pane beside it. The commands (`tile_windows_grid`, `split_pane_right`, `split_pane_down`, `open_second_view`, `move_tab_to_other_pane`, `close_pane`, `sync_pane_zoom`, `equalize_panes`) are in the palette and on the agent channel; Untile merges the panes. The split and its sizes are kept per project and come back when the project is reopened, and a workspace layout can store Tile Grid as its split.
