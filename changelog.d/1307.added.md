- **Document panes (#1307)** — drag a document tab onto an edge of the document area to move it into a new pane there, or hold Ctrl (Cmd on macOS) to open a second view of it. View ▸ Window has Split Right, Split Down and Open Second View (`split_pane_right`, `split_pane_down`, `open_second_view`, also on the agent channel); Untile merges the panes. The split is kept per project and comes back when the project is reopened.
