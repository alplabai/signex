- **Re-prefix placed parts to the project's designator standard, and a per-row prefix override (#1315)** — a **Re-prefix to standard** button in the Annotate dialog re-issues the prefix of every part placed from a library row under the project's standard and renumbers it, as one undo step across the open sheets. Locked, hand-edited, unlinked, unmounted and read-only parts are left alone and listed in the Messages panel. A library row can pin its own letters with a `designator_prefix` text parameter, which both placement paths and Re-prefix honour.
