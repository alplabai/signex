- **DRC fixes from the command line (#2940)** — `fix_drc document=<board> [rule=<kind>]` fixes every finding of one rule kind (clearance, track width, annular ring, hole to hole, silkscreen to pad, solder mask sliver) and `fix_drc_violation document=<board> violation=<key>` fixes one finding, as one undo step. A fix never waives a finding and never changes a locked track, via or part; a finding it cannot fix, or whose fix would make a new finding, is left and the answer says why. `dry_run=true` answers with the fixes and changes nothing; the answer lists what was fixed, what was left and what a check finds afterwards.
