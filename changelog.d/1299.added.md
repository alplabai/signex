- **Export Jobs and PNG pages (#1299)** — an Export Job (`.snxout`, format `snxout/1`) lists a project's outputs (schematic PDF, PNG pages at a chosen resolution, BOM, netlist) and the folder they go to; `run_output_job` writes every enabled output, or one by name, from the command palette or headless with `signex-cli`, and two runs write the same files. `export_sheet_png` writes a schematic's pages as PNG images. The rules are in `docs/OUTPUT_JOBS.md`.
