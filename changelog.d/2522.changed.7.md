- **A pin end on wires of two nets gets no junction** — when a moved part's pin end lands where two overlapping wires of different nets pass, with or without "Keep wires connected", no junction dot is added there, so the two nets stay apart, and Messages names the pin and both nets: "could not join R1 pin 1 to the wires it ends on: they carry two nets, N$1 and N$2".
