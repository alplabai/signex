- **A pin end on wires of two nets gets no junction** — when a pin end lands where wires of different nets meet (two wires overlapping or crossing, or a wire ending there in a T with no dot), whether the part or the wires moved and with or without "Keep wires connected", no junction dot is added there, so the nets stay apart, and Messages names the pin and both nets: "could not join R1 pin 1 to the wires it ends on: they carry two nets, N$1 and N$2". Repair Pin Junctions leaves such a touch alone too.
