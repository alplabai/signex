- **Gerber Viewer tab (#2539)** — a read-only document tab shows a fabrication package: a folder, a zip read in place (never unpacked to disk) or picked files. The layer list puts one row per file in drawing order with a grip to reorder, an eye, a colour swatch and a badge naming where the layer's name came from (X2, the job file or the file name); files that are not fabrication data are listed as Not read. The board view zooms at the pointer, pans by dragging and selects by click; Properties shows the selected object's type, file and line, D-code, aperture, X2 net, pin and component. The toolbar has Open (Ctrl+O on the tab), Flip, Drills, mm / mil and Highlight net. With nothing open the tab offers Open folder…, Open zip package… and Open files…, names the files it reads, and lists the last three of ten recent packages, a moved one greyed with "Files not found". A double-click on a `.gbr`, `.drl` or `.zip` row in Projects, or files dropped on a window, open the tab.
