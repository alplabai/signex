- **Named unions (#2655)** — select objects and press `Ctrl+G` (or right-click ▸ Unions ▸ Create Union…) to group them under a name and tags. A union shows as a dashed outline with a name tab; clicking a member selects the whole union. The new Unions panel (View ▸ Unions) filters by name or `#tag`, lists the sheet's or the project's unions, and locks, hides, renames, adds or removes members and breaks a union. A locked union's members cannot be moved or edited; hiding fades the members on the canvas only and is not an undo step.
