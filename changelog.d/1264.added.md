- **Paste array and rubber-stamp copy (#1264)** — `rubber_stamp_copy` copies the selection and keeps it on the cursor, one undo step per click, until Esc or a right-click. `paste_array` (command palette) opens a dialog for the clipboard with copies (1 to 1000), X and Y spacing in the active unit and a text increment step; OK places the copies as one undo step, copy k offset by k spacings with its label numbers moved k steps and its designators following the Designators on paste preference.
