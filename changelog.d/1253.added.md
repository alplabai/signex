- **Repeat placement with auto-increment (#1253)** — a net label, port or text note placed again and again counts up its trailing number (`D0`, `D1`, `D2`, keeping zero padding such as `A07`, `A08`) by the new Increment step in Preferences > Editing (default 1, -100 to 100, never 0). Repeat last item (`repeat_last_item`) arms the last label or note again with the next value, and says so in the Messages panel when nothing was placed yet.
