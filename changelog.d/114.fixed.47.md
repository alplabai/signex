- **Power-port text is fixed to its port (#114)** — a power port's net name can no longer be dragged away from the port: a click on it selects the whole port, the selection halo covers both symbol and text, and the text follows the port's move, rotate and mirror. Files with a custom text offset still open and keep it on disk; it is no longer drawn. A double-click on the port edits its net name.
