- **A click inside a PCB zone cut-out no longer counts as a hit on the zone (#899)** — `Zone::contains_point` tests a point against the outline and every hole that is drawn as a cut-out, so the hit-test agrees with both render paths: a point in a hole misses, copper around the hole and a hole that is not drawn as a cut-out (fewer than 3 vertices, outside the outline, overlapping) still hit.
