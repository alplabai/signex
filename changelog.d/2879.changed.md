- **Board Snap Options: new defaults, a length for Snap Distance, edge and intersection snapping (#2879)** — a new session snaps to every supported object kind with Axes on (it was Pad Centers and Via Centers, Axes off); Snap Distance is a length in the board's unit, 1 mm by default and never under 8 screen pixels (it was a multiple of the snap grid), and Axis Snap Range defaults to 2 mm (was 10 mm). Current Layer now snaps to texts, drawings and regions on the active layer as well as copper. Regions/Polygons/Fills and Board Shape snap to the nearest point of their edges, footprint drawings count as Track/Arc Lines, and Intersections include arcs, circles, region edges and the board edge. Shift+E on a board is the new `cycle_board_snapping` command in both built-in profiles; `toggle_electrical_grid` no longer steps Snapping on a board. The snap options are not saved, so nothing carries over from the old Distance setting.
