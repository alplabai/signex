- **ERC reports unconnected sheet pins and conflicting sheet-pin outputs (#1836)** — two new waivable rules, `sheet_pin_unconnected` and `sheet_pin_direction_conflict` (Warning), flag a sheet symbol pin with nothing wired to it and two `output` sheet pins on one net, and the "not exposed as a sheet pin" finding now names the parent sheet symbol. A waiver made before this change on that finding for a child placed by more than one sheet symbol stops matching once and the finding reappears; single-placement waivers are unaffected.
