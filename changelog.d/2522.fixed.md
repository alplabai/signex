- **Routes and tracks start on the active layer** — Interactive Routing, Quick Route and Track started on a pad or copper that is not on the active copper layer (a surface pad on Top with an inner layer active) take that copper's net and start with a via there, in the same undo step as the first leg; a through-hole pad starts directly. A leg, track run or route that ends on a pad off its layer ends with a via. Before, such a start took no net and nothing said why.
- **A route that ends on a pad of no net gives the pad its net** — the pad joins the route's net, locked, in the route's undo step; the status bar says so ("U1.1 joined net A") and Update PCB lists the pad as keeping a net set on the board instead of clearing it. A leg aimed at a pad of another net is refused, and the status bar and Messages name the pad and both nets.
- **Differential Pair and Multi-Route start with vias** — a net whose copper is not on the active layer starts with a via, as Interactive Routing does.
- **Pours say why they have no net or no fill** — a Polygon Pour takes the net set in Properties, else the net under its first corner on copper. A pour placed with no net, or one the fill leaves empty, says why in the status bar and Messages, and the pour's Properties says why it has no fill.
