- **Trapezoid and custom pads (#2242)** — a pad can be a trapezoid (one pair of edges longer than the other by a delta) or a polygon of its own. Both turn with the pad and its footprint and are mirrored on the bottom side, and the canvas, hit-testing, the ratsnest, pours, the design rule check and Gerber output all read the same outline. The footprint editor's pad shape list offers Trapezoid, with Delta X and Delta Y rows; a library custom pad now places on the board instead of being refused.
- **`set_pad_shape` (#2242)** — an agent or script sets a board pad's shape with its parameters (`shape`, `delta` for a trapezoid, `corners` for a custom pad, pad-local mm) as one undo step; parameters that make no copper are refused.
