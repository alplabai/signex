- **Fab tier profile cards (#2537)** — the setup cards of New Project, New PCB and the Project setup section are now the fab tiers **Standard**, **Advanced** and **HDI**, plus **Custom**, in a 2 x 2 grid; Hobby 2-layer is gone. The copper layer count is its own stepper on every card (2 to 32, in steps of 2) and no longer switches the profile to Custom. The tiers carry one rule table (Standard 0.15 / 0.2 mm, Advanced 0.1 / 0.125 mm, HDI 0.075 / 0.075 mm clearance / track), and the Customize field "Manufacturer rule template" is now **Fab tier**, which mirrors the picked card.
