- **Computed BOM columns (#1988)** — a BOM column can be an `=` expression over a line's fields, its sheet's and the project's variables (`= VCC^2 / Value`, `= Quantity * UnitPrice`), shown and exported with its derived unit. A grouped line whose parts give different results says so, and a cell that fails is empty and named once in Messages. A BOM template (`snxbomt/1`) keeps a set of columns, computed ones included, and `bom_lines` answers with a project's BOM lines under a template as JSON.
