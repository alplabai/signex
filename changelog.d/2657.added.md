- **Harnesses pass through child sheets (#2657)** — a child sheet that only passes a signal harness on (a harness port and a harness-typed sheet entry, no connector) now carries it to its own child: the signals connect entry by entry, nested harnesses included. ERC compares the parent's connector with the connector the harness reaches and reports a missing entry only where the harness really stops. See `docs/SIGNAL_HARNESSES.md`.
