- **Dim other nets (#2514)** — Preferences ▸ PCB ▸ Display has a Dim other nets slider, 0 to 100 %, 50 % by default. While Highlight Net shows a net, while you route a track, drag or move objects, paste or place a part, or place a via, pad, arc, track or copper area on a set net, the copper of every other net is drawn that much fainter; the nets you are working on stay at full brightness. 0 % leaves the board as it is, 100 % hides the other copper. The board comes back when the highlight or the tool ends, Esc included. The amount is saved with your preferences.
