- **Dim other nets while a net is highlighted (#2514)** — Preferences ▸ PCB ▸ Display has a Dim other nets slider, 0 to 100 %, 50 % by default. While Highlight Net shows a net on the board, the tracks, arcs, vias, pads and zones of every other net are drawn that much fainter; the highlighted net stays at full brightness. 0 % leaves the board as it is, 100 % hides the other copper. Esc or Highlight Net again brings the board back. The amount is saved with your preferences.
