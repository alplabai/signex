- **Calculated values everywhere (#1986)** — the BOM, the netlist export, ERC rules and the Net Inspector now use a part's calculated value, as the sheet and the PDF do, and the BOM groups parts by it. A reference such as `= R5.Value` now stays on its part through Reset Designators, annotation and designator changes or swaps made by hand. Under the full-codes designator standard, typing the shown `-` sign in a reference (`= -QAC1.Value`) is an error instead of a silent negation. The Net Inspector's By Component tab shows designators as the project's standard shows them.
