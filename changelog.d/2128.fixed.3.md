- **Zooming a sheet with rotated text is 2-4x faster (#2128)** — the iced fork (release iced-signex-v0.15.0-alpha.5) now tessellates each rotated glyph once per zoom step and reuses it, instead of on every frame; on a large test sheet a zoom frame on the GPU path went from 35 ms to 20 ms at fit and a fractional pan from 12.6 ms to 4.9 ms, with memory unchanged.
