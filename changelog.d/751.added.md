- **Sync Sheet Pins (#359)** — right-clicking a selected sheet symbol now offers "Sync Sheet Pins", which reconciles its pins with the child schematic's Hierarchical and Global labels (live tab if open, else the file on disk) through `Command::ReconcileChildSheetPins`, so it is one undo step and refreshes the netlist. A sync never moves an existing pin: matched pins keep their position and direction (so attached wires keep their nets), hand-added pins that match a label stay hand-added and in place, new pins take a slot free of other pins, of pins dropped in the same sync and of the parent sheet's wires, labels, junctions and no-connects, with their label's shape as direction (bidirectional if none), and the auto-generated pins of removed labels are dropped. The Messages panel reports which pins were added and removed, any port left without a pin because the symbol edge has no free slot, plus a missing, unparseable or port-less child. Port names are trimmed the same way by the sync, the project netlist stitcher and the ERC hierarchy rule.
