- **Measure, Find, Find Similar and the Net Inspector work on a board (#2403)** — Measure Distance reads point to point and, between two objects, edge to edge, with snapping and a ruler on the canvas. Find and Replace searches a board's designators, values, footprint names and nets, and replaces designators and values as one undo step. Find Similar Objects selects footprints, pads, tracks, vias and areas that match the seed. The Net Inspector lists a board's nets with routed length, vias, pads and routed state, and Select Net and Highlight Net act on a board net. Agents read the same with `measure_board`, `query_board_nets` and `find_similar_items`.
