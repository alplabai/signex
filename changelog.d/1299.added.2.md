- **Export Job Gerber settings (#1299)** — a Gerber output of an Export Job chooses its units (millimetres or inches), number format, zero suppression, the layers it writes and whether every aperture is a macro, in its form or with the `set_job_gerber_*` commands. Left as they are, the settings write the same files File ▸ Fabrication Outputs writes. The rules are in `docs/OUTPUT_JOBS.md`, "Gerber settings".
