- **Number Schematic Sheets and Tile windows menu rows work (#2247)** — Tools ▸ Annotation ▸ Number Schematic Sheets opens a dialog that lists the project's pages in hierarchy order with their sheet numbers. Type a number to give a page its own, or Auto-number to put every page back in order; Apply changes the sheets as one undo step and nothing is saved until you save. Window ▸ Tile Horizontally and Tile Vertically arrange the main window and the undocked document windows side by side or stacked on the main window's screen. The new `number_schematic_sheets`, `tile_windows_horizontally` and `tile_windows_vertically` commands are also in the command palette and can be given a key.
