- **Footprint Pad Properties show what the pad has (#2577)** — an SMD pad shows only its own layer: one "Pad · Top Layer" (or Bottom Layer) section with its shape, size, corner radius, copper offset and that layer's paste and solder mask, without the pad stack modes or the other side. Each paste and solder mask layer is a switch over a Rule | Manual expansion (the solder mask switch is on while the mask opens over the pad, replacing the "tented" checkbox). Through-hole pads and holes show both masks and no paste; a non-plated hole ("NPTH") hides the pad stack, copper offset, electrical type and thermal relief. The copper table names inner copper by position ("Inner", or L2…Ln−1 in Full Stack) instead of "Mid Layer". The header shows the pad's kind as a pill and links to Edit in Sketch, the preview is a line-art drawing whose callouts light up with their fields, the hole is a Round | Slot switch, and Pad Features start closed.
