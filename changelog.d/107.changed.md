- **A pin end that lands on a wire connects to it** — placing or moving a part so a pin end lands inside a wire, or drawing or moving a wire through a pin end, adds a junction dot there in the same undo step, so the pin joins that net, unless "Keep wires connected" holds the moved pin on its own net (it then keeps that net and Messages says so). A sheet that already has pins touching a wire with no dot is left as it is when opened; Messages names how many, and Repair Pin Junctions adds the dots as one undo step.
