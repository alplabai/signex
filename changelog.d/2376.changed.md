- **Schematic Active Bar and its menus follow the Graphite layout** — the 13 group buttons sit in Select / Place / Annotate groups behind thin separators, each button that opens a menu has a corner triangle, and the Harness group, whose tools are all planned, is greyed and opens nothing. While a wire or bus is drawn, a 90° / 45° / Any pill picks the corner mode. The Power menu lists its ports under Common, Styles and Grounds with short names, each drawn with its own port glyph in the power colour, and keeps the full "Place … power port" text as the tooltip. The Net Color menu shows its swatches in the icon column at the menu's body size. The Selection Filter dropdown has a title row with All / None, the kind chips, and its presets with a new "Save current" button. Menu rows show the active shortcut profile's keys.
- **Sheet right-click menu is shorter and starts with what it stands on** — a muted first line names the sheet or the selection. On an empty spot it holds Find, Place, Sheet Actions, Undo, Redo, Paste, Paste Special, Select All, Net Color, Project Options and Preferences; Find Similar and Highlight Net appear when the click hits an object, Clear Filter while a net highlight is on. With a selection it opens on Properties (the bold default row), then the edits, transforms, flyouts and net rows, Undo, and a greyed "Planned (N)" flyout holding the rows not built yet. The Place flyout groups its rows under subheads, shows short power port names in the power colour, and gathers the planned tools in one greyed row.
- **Grid picker** — the ladder sits under Imperial and Metric subheads, mil steps show their mm value, the active step is filled as well as checked, a last row opens Grid Properties, and a small preview beside it shows whether parts on the 2.54 mm pin pitch land on the step.
