- **Project requirements as checks (#2339)** — a project can list its own requirements in the `.snxprj` (ERC clean, DRC clean, a maximum board size, approved part lifecycle states, net class membership and minimums, a BOM cost budget at a build quantity), and the new `check_requirements` query runs them on demand from `signex-cli` or an agent, answering in the shared check report with a finding per unmet requirement, the met ones listed as passed, and a comparison with an earlier run.
