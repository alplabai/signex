- **Rooms from Sheets (#2457)** — a board can hold rooms: named areas for the parts of one schematic sheet, or one channel of a sheet placed several times. **Design ▸ Rooms ▸ Create Rooms from Sheets…** plans a room per sheet (`name_CH1…CHN` for repeats) round its parts or in an even grid, previews them, and on Create draws them and packs the parts Update PCB left beside the board into their rooms, all as one undo step. **Place ▸ Room** draws one by two corners. Room Properties edits the name, sheet, side, shape, position and size (moving a room moves its parts), shows the parts inside and outside, and has the **Keep parts inside** rule with a margin; the new DRC rule `builtin::part_outside_room` reports a part outside its room. Rooms are saved as a `[[rooms]]` array in the `.snxpcb`, only when a board has rooms.
