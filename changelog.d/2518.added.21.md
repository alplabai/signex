- **Slot and Polygon in Sketch ▸ Create (#2518)** — after Point, on footprint and board sketches. Slot: click both centres, then a third click sets the width; it places two arcs joined by two lines with the centre distance and the width as dimensions (a typed dimension input is the centre distance). Polygon: click the centre and the first vertex; the dimension input is the side count (3 to 64, empty is 6). Each shape is one undo step and bakes as any closed profile.
- **Snaps that add their constraint (#2518)** — while drawing, the cursor snaps to a line's midpoint (◐) and a circle's or arc's centre (⊙) and the click adds a Midpoint or a Coincident; an intersection snap adds a point-on-curve for both curves. Snap ▾ has switches for midpoints and centres. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on macOS) while drawing to place a point with no inferred constraint, the line's automatic horizontal / vertical included.
