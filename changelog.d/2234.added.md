- **Board sketch storage, bake and keepout DRC (#2234)** — a board can now hold a parametric sketch, saved in the `.snxpcb` in a new optional `[board_sketch]` section, that bakes to the board outline, keepouts, cutouts, mounting holes (with drill, plating and net) and V-scores; a sketch that does not close or does not solve is refused with an error that names the problem. Committing it is one undo step, and Design Rule Check now reports copper, vias, tracks and footprints inside a keepout that forbids them. Boards without a sketch save exactly as before. The sketch-mode editor comes next.
