- **Footprint sketch edits no longer stall on the redundancy check (#1685)** — on a dense sketch the check for constraints that restate others could freeze the editor for several hundred milliseconds after an edit; it now runs in the background and the "redundant" markers appear a moment after the solve, and are dropped if you have edited again meanwhile. A solve that runs outside an editor event (saving, the silk and library panels) shows its markers at the next footprint event. The background check gets its own full work budget, so a sketch that used to report "redundancy check skipped (budget)" may now be checked in full.
