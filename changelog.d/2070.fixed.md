- **Applying a library update no longer overwrites a part you edited meanwhile (#2070)** — a footprint, Manufacturer or MPN changed while Apply (or "Update from library") was running now keeps your edit, on every unit of a multi-unit part, and Messages names the part and field it left alone (no line when you typed the row's new value). The skipped field's base still advances to the row, so your edit reads as an override and a later row change raises a conflict, and the part's version moves with the update. A part moved to another row meanwhile (Change Component) is skipped whole, with a Messages line.
