- **Status bar in three clusters, and a Grid & Snap popover (#2262)** — the status bar reads cursor · grid and snap · tool and hint, separated by faint rules. X and Y show numbers only, with the unit once after Y (click it to change the unit), and the Grid read-out always names its unit. Right-click a schematic's Grid read-out to open the Grid & Snap popover, with a small drawing of the grid and switches for Visible Grid, Snap Grid and Snap to Hotspots; it closes when the window shows another document or a dialog opens. A left click still toggles the grid. The Panels list groups the panels under Project, Inspect, Verify, Library and PCB, ticks the open ones and says where each is docked.
