- **Wire and bus corner: flip and corner modes (#2372)** — while drawing a wire or bus, Space flips the corner (horizontal first or vertical first) and Shift+Space steps the corner mode through 90°, 45° start, 45° end and any angle. The preview changes at once, the committed segment follows it, and the choice holds for later segments until it is changed again. The status bar names the current corner. Nothing is added to the undo history until a segment is placed. In the Altium profile the keys reach this through Rotate (they still rotate outside a wire run); the Classic profile uses `/` and `Shift+Space`, and both actions are in the command catalog as "Flip the wire/bus corner" and "Cycle the wire/bus corner mode".
