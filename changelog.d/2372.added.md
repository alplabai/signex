- **Wire and bus corner: flip and corner modes (#2372)** — while drawing a wire or bus, "Flip the wire/bus corner" (`switch_segment_posture`) swaps horizontal-first for vertical-first, and "Cycle the wire/bus corner mode" (`cycle_wire_bus_graphic_mode`) steps through 90°, 45° start, 45° end and any angle. The preview changes at once, the committed segment follows it, and the choice holds for later segments until it is changed again. The status bar names the current corner. Nothing is added to the undo history until a segment is placed. In the Classic profile the keys are `/` and `Shift+Space`; the Altium profile does not bind them yet, because `Space` and `Shift+Space` rotate there.
