- **Back Annotate (#1312)** — Tools ▸ Annotation ▸ Back Annotate Schematics... and the new Design ▸ Update Schematics in `<project>` list the board's designators and values that differ from the schematic, matched by each footprint's link to its part. Tick the changes to carry back, Validate them, and Execute applies them to every changed sheet as one undo step; sheets that are not open are changed in memory and marked unsaved, and nothing is saved until you save. A designator change on a sheet placed more than once applies to that placement only; a value change there, a change for an unannotated or duplicated schematic designator, and a different footprint (until #2248) are listed but not applied, with the reason. The `back_annotate_schematics` command is also in the command palette and can be given a key.
