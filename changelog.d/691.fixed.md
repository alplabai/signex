- **Multi-unit parts are one part in annotation, ERC and the BOM (#691)** — units of one part (same designator and library symbol, different unit) now share one designator when annotated, are no longer flagged as a duplicate designator by ERC, and count as one BOM row with quantity 1. All three share one grouping rule in `signex-types`. The Protel netlist export still treats units as duplicate designators and is tracked separately.
