- **Menus open at the cursor and draw on top (#114)** — a right-click menu now opens with its corner at the cursor in every canvas, panel and undocked window, and moves only as far as needed to stay inside that window; it used to jump over the menu bar because its size was guessed from a fixed row count. Submenu flyouts sit level with the row that opens them. An open menu-bar dropdown now covers the docks and panels under it instead of letting their labels, icons and the canvas show through. On the Graphite themes every menu, flyout and dropdown uses one card: 13 px type on 28 px rows, a 16 px monochrome icon column, a right-aligned mono shortcut column, a chevron column, soft separators, a 10 px radius with the floating shadow, and a width fitted to its rows between 200 and 320 px; the click area of a row is exactly the row drawn. The grid picker uses the same rows, with a check mark on the active step.
