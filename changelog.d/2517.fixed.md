- **Board sketch patterns bake every instance (#2517)** — a linear, rectangular or circular pattern in the board sketch now bakes each instance as its own mounting hole, slot, cutout, keepout, pour, V-score or drawing, instead of one object. A board outline or component anchor in a pattern is refused by name, and a sketch whose patterns place more than 10000 items is refused with a Messages line naming the limit and the item that went over.
