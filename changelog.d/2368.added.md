- **Designator and comment text on its own (#2368)** — click a component's designator or comment on the board to select it (hovering shows its box), and drag it to move it without the part, in one undo step; a locked part keeps its text. A box or Select All still takes the part, and Move and Measure still act on the part under the text. With the Texts selection filter off, a press on a text starts a box. The text keeps a justification (left, centre or right of its anchor), saved with the board, the board print shows designators and comments where the board does, and agents place, turn, size and justify the text with `set_footprint_text`.
