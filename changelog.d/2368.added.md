- **Footprint designator and comment text on the board (#2368)** — the board canvas draws each component's designator and comment on its own layer, in that layer's colour, at the text's position, rotation and size from the board. The text moves, turns and flips with its component (mirrored on the bottom side), follows it during a drag, hides with its layer, in single-layer mode, and when the Component panel's eye is closed. Both render modes draw it the same way.
