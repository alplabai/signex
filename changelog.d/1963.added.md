- **Clicking a pin of a placed part shows that pin in Properties (#1963)** — a click at a pin's line or end selects the part, and its Properties panel opens the Pins section with that pin's row highlighted. The section is a table of pin, name, electrical type, net and hidden; the net comes from the project net index (the same one the hover card reads) once it is built. A click on the body, or anywhere inside it, keeps the part-level view, and pins the symbol hides are listed but cannot be picked. Pin rows are read-only on a placed part.
