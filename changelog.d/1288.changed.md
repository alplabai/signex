- **ERC panel redesign (#1288)** — Run ERC is a full-width button with its key (F8) and reads "Running…" while it runs; Clear moved to a trash icon in the panel's title bar. Error, warning and info counts are tiles that list only their severity, findings are grouped by rule with a count and fold, and each row shows its message, then its sheet and position. Show, Add No-Connect, Waive net and Waive appear on hover or selection; the previous / next arrows are gone (click a row instead). A clean run says how many sheets, symbols and nets it checked and when, and every stored waiver stays in a Waived group at the end with its status: waived, waives N findings, LAPSED, matches no finding, or ignored. The waiver form takes a multi-line Reason and an Author that starts as your git or login name.
- A finished ERC run selects no finding; pick a row to see its actions.
