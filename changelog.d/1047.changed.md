- **Footprint sketch drags no longer freeze the editor while the solver runs (#1047)** — a drag tick moves the geometry at once and solves it on a background task; a result is applied only if no newer edit has happened since, and ticks that arrive meanwhile are coalesced into one follow-up solve. A save, a footprint switch or any edit that records its own undo step first finishes the owed solve, so nothing unsolved is ever written. Other sketch edits still solve inline. The constraint colours, baked pads and a conflict rejection now land a moment after the drag tick instead of within it.
