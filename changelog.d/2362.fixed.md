- **Board layer colours (#2362)** — the Board Outline is drawn and listed in magenta, and Keep-Out takes a paler, pinker magenta so the two are told apart. Each technical layer has its own colour: Adhesive, Fab, Assembly, Courtyard, 3D Body, Fiducial and Designator each get a related top and bottom pair with the top brighter, and Dimensions, Notes, Drill Drawing, ECO 1 and ECO 2 each get their own. Copper, overlay, solder, paste and the Multi-Layer keep their colours. Every board layer colour has at least 3:1 contrast against the board area and the workspace.
