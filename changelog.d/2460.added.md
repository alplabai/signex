- **Board counters in the status bar (#2460)** — on a board the status bar shows Routed (routed of all connections, a progress bar and the percent), Unrouted, DRC (the last run's violations and how long ago it ran) and Selected. Each counter opens a popover with a click, and the active layer pill and the grid value open theirs with a right-click (a click still steps the layer and shows or hides the grid): pick the active layer, pick or type a grid pitch, see the unrouted connections by net and route the next one, zoom to a DRC violation or run DRC again, and zoom to, inspect or clear the selection.
