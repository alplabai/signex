- **Assign Footprints and Net Inspector menu rows work (#2248, #1269)** — Tools ▸ Assign Footprints opens a table of every placed part in the project with its designator, value, library row and footprint. Each part can take only the footprints its own library row offers; tick several parts to set them at once. Apply changes the open sheets as one undo step and nothing is saved until you save; parts on sheets that are not open are listed only. Tools ▸ Net Inspector opens a panel listing every net of the project with its net class and pin count. Filter it, expand a net to see its pins, labels, ports and sheets, and click one to go to it with the net highlighted. The new `assign_footprints` and `open_net_inspector` commands are also in the command palette and can be given a key.
