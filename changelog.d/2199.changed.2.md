- **For headless callers: `place_component`, `place_sheet_symbol`, `place_sheet_entry` and `sync_sheet_pins` now run (#2199)** — they were refused headless as window-only commands; now they take operands, a headless call without them is refused with the operand it needs, and `sync_sheet_pins` is an undoable document edit. In a window, without operands, nothing changes: Place Part opens the picker, the sheet tools arm and Sync Sheet Pins acts on the selection.
