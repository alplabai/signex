- **Net class conflicts and directives on paper (#2656)** — when two directives give one net different net classes (two Net Parameters directives, a Net Parameters directive over a Net Blanket, or two Net Blankets), the net still takes the class of the first in rank (Net Parameters before Net Blankets, each in sheet order) and a new ERC warning, Net class conflict, names the overridden directive and the winner. The finding can be waived.
