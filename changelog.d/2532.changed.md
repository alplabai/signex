- **Pre-placement form with editable Position (#2532)** — the `Tab` form of every placement tool takes a typed Position X / Y (Y from the sheet's page origin, a wire's Start and End) and places the next object there on **OK** or `Enter` as one undo step, while untyped fields follow the cursor and a typed position is used once. The form gets the Graphite look: a key caption under the header, Rotation as a 0° / 90° / 180° / 270° control, a justification grid that names its anchor, wire Width as a 2 × 2 preset grid, a read-only wire Length, line-art illustrations for the net label and wire tools, and a "No other options" card for tools such as the junction.
