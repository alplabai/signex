- **More DRC rule kinds (#2435)** — the rule language and the Rules tab gain hole rules (`on hole`: hole size, annular ring, hole-to-hole spacing), board edge clearance (`on edge`), via-in-pad (`on via require pad_gap`), silkscreen to pad and to silkscreen (`on silk`), solder mask slivers and expansions (`on mask`), paste expansions (`on paste`) and courtyard overlap (`on courtyard`); Run DRC and `check_drc` report each, the Rules tab has Holes, Mask & paste and Silkscreen & courtyard sub-tabs, and the Routing sub-tab gains a Min pad gap column. A via drill under both a via `drill` minimum and a hole `diameter` minimum is one finding, and a finding on an object of no net says "No net".
