- **Drag an unselected footprint on a board in one gesture (#2358)** — pressing on a footprint that is not selected, or on one of its pads, and dragging now selects it and moves it with the pointer, snapping to the grid; the release is one undo step. A plain click still only selects, a press on empty space still draws a selection box, and a locked footprint is selected but stays put, with the reason in Messages.
