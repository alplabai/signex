- **Power ports use the power colour, and every stroke follows one width rule (#2091)** — A power port draws its body, pin and Value text in the theme's power colour on the canvas and in SVG / PDF. A stored stroke of 0 now draws at 0.254 mm for drawings, child sheet frames and the placed-wire, bus-entry and power-port previews too, and an explicit width is floored at 0.15 mm; the SVG junction follows the canvas radius. New built-in power ports have a stock-pin-long stem and larger heads; the connection point does not move and saved sheets keep their own symbol. Sheets that embedded a stock GND, VCC, +3V3 or +5V copy show the library-update notice for it, since the library symbol changed; no symbol version was bumped.
